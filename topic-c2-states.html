📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Matter = has mass + takes up space. 4 states: solid, liquid, gas, plasma.物质＝有质量＋占空间。4 种状态：固、液、气、等离子体。
  • Solid: definite shape and volume. Liquid: indefinite shape, definite volume. Gas: both indefinite.固体：形状体积都固定。液体：形状不定、体积固定。气体：都不固定。
  • Only gases are compressible (particles far apart).只有气体可压缩（粒子相距远）。

💡 Push the plunger of a sealed syringe full of air and it slides in. Fill it with water and it will not budge. That one difference — how closely the particles are packed — explains every row of the states-of-matter table.推一支装满空气、封住口的针筒，活塞能推进去；装满水就推不动。就这一个差别——粒子挤得有多紧——解释了物质状态表的每一行。

  1. Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space (volume). Light, heat and sound are energy, not matter.物质＝有质量并占据空间（体积）的任何东西。光、热、声是能量，不是物质。
  2. Matter has 4 common states: solid, liquid, gas and plasma (very hot, charged gas — lightning, the Sun).物质有 4 种常见状态：固体、液体、气体和等离子体（极热、带电的气体——闪电、太阳）。
  3. Solid: definite shape, definite volume, particles packed tightly in place, not compressible. Liquid: indefinite shape (takes the container's shape), definite volume, particles touching but sliding past each other, not compressible.固体：形状固定、体积固定，粒子紧密排列、不能移动位置，不可压缩。液体：形状不固定（随容器）、体积固定，粒子相互接触但能彼此滑动，不可压缩。
  4. Gas: indefinite shape and indefinite volume (fills any container), particles far apart and touching only when they collide, compressible.气体：形状和体积都不固定（充满任何容器），粒子相距很远、只在碰撞时接触，可压缩。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes list 4 common states including plasma, and say liquids are not compressible. Many textbooks list only 3 everyday states and say liquids are "nearly" incompressible. On this test: 4 states, and only gases are compressible.课堂讲义列出包括等离子体在内的 4 种常见状态，并说液体不可压缩。很多教材只列 3 种日常状态，说液体「几乎」不可压缩。考试按讲义：4 种状态，只有气体可压缩。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Memorise the 3 × 4 table (shape, volume, particle arrangement, compressible) — it is often a fill-in-the-blank. Use the words definite / indefinite exactly.背熟 3 × 4 表（形状、体积、粒子排列、可否压缩）——常考填空。用词要准确：definite（固定）／indefinite（不固定）。

Example 1例 1L1 Know知道
Fill in: a solid has a ___ shape and a ___ volume; a liquid has an ___ shape and a ___ volume; a gas has an ___ shape and an ___ volume.填空：固体形状___、体积___；液体形状___、体积___；气体形状___、体积___。
  1. An ice cube keeps its shape and size → definite shape, definite volume.冰块保持形状和大小→形状固定、体积固定。
  2. Juice poured into a glass takes the glass's shape but stays 250 mL → indefinite shape, definite volume.果汁倒进杯子会变成杯子的形状，但仍是 250 mL→形状不固定、体积固定。
  3. Air spreads to fill the whole room → indefinite shape, indefinite volume.空气扩散充满整个房间→形状不固定、体积也不固定。
Answer答案
solid: definite, definite; liquid: indefinite, definite; gas: indefinite, indefinite固体：形状固定、体积固定；液体：形状不固定、体积固定；气体：形状不固定、体积不固定

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
A sealed syringe of air can be pushed in, but a sealed syringe of water cannot. Explain using particles.封口的空气针筒能推进去，封口的水针筒却推不动。用粒子解释。
  1. Air particles are far apart, touching only when they collide — lots of empty space.空气粒子相距很远，只在碰撞时接触——有大量空隙。
  2. Pushing the plunger moves the particles closer together → the gas is compressed.推活塞让粒子靠得更近→气体被压缩。
  3. Water particles are already in close contact — no room to push them closer → not compressible.水分子已经紧挨着——没有空间再靠近→不可压缩。
  1. A gas has an indefinite volume, so its volume can be changed by squeezing.气体体积不固定，所以挤压可以改变它的体积。
  2. A liquid has a definite volume, so squeezing cannot make it smaller.液体体积固定，所以挤压不能让它变小。
Answer答案
Gas particles are far apart (space to squeeze); liquid particles already touch.气体粒子相距很远（有空间可压缩）；液体粒子已经紧挨着。
🧭 What this example trains这道题练什么
🎯 Goal考查目标
Explain compressibility from how far apart particles are.用粒子间距解释可压缩性。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Saying a liquid has an indefinite volume because it "changes shape". Shape changes, volume does not: 250 mL stays 250 mL in any glass.因为液体「会变形」就说它体积不固定。变的是形状，体积不变：250 mL 倒进哪个杯子都是 250 mL。
  • Calling light or heat matter. They have no mass and take up no space — they are energy.把光或热当作物质。它们没有质量、不占空间——是能量。
  • Forgetting plasma when asked for the 4 common states.问 4 种常见状态时忘了等离子体。

7L4 Solve会想🌍 Modelling建模📘 Concepts概念理解🔍 Checking & reasoning检验与推理
A scuba tank holds enough air to fill a whole room, yet the tank is small. A tank this size could never hold a room's worth of water. Explain both facts using the particle model.一个潜水气瓶装的空气能充满整个房间，可气瓶却很小。同样大小的瓶子绝不可能装下一房间的水。用粒子模型解释这两个事实。
Which state is compressible, and why?哪种状态可压缩，为什么？
Gas particles are far apart, so air can be compressed into the small tank and expands to fill a room when released. Water particles already touch, so water cannot be compressed — a room's worth of water keeps its large volume.气体粒子相距很远，空气能被压进小瓶，放出时又膨胀充满房间。水分子已经紧挨着，水不能被压缩——一房间的水体积还是那么大。
  1. Gas: lots of space between particles → squeezed into the tank.气体：粒子间空隙大→可压进气瓶。
  2. Liquid: particles in close contact → definite volume, not compressible.液体：粒子紧密接触→体积固定，不可压缩。
