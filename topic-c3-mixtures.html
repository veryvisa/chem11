📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Homogeneous = 1 phase; heterogeneous = more than 1 phase = mechanical mixture.均匀＝1 个相；不均匀＝不止 1 个相＝机械混合物。
  • Homogeneous splits: pure substance (constant composition) vs solution (variable composition).均匀物质再分：纯净物（组成固定）与溶液（组成可变）。
  • Pure splits: element (1 type of atom) vs compound (more than 1). Solute dissolves in solvent.纯净物再分：元素（1 种原子）与化合物（不止 1 种）。溶质溶于溶剂。

💡 Salt water, trail mix, copper, sugar, air — every one lands in exactly one box of the classification tree. Learn the two questions that walk you down the tree and this whole section is marks.盐水、什锦坚果、铜、糖、空气——每一样都正好落在分类树的一个格子里。学会沿着树往下走的两个问题，这一整节就是送分。

  1. Question 1 — how many phases? Homogeneous = one phase, looks the same everywhere (air, water, salt water, a piece of iron). Heterogeneous = more than one phase, you can see different parts (gravel, sand in water, a pencil, salad).第一问——几个相？均匀的＝一个相，处处看起来一样（空气、水、盐水、一块铁）。不均匀的＝不止一个相，能看出不同部分（碎石、沙和水、铅笔、沙拉）。
  2. Question 2 (homogeneous only) — fixed composition? Pure substance = one substance, fixed formula (HX2​O, OX2​, Cu, sugar). Homogeneous mixture = solution = 2+ substances physically combined, amounts can vary (salt water, air, soda pop, water and alcohol).第二问（只对均匀的）——组成固定吗？纯净物＝一种物质、化学式固定（HX2​O、OX2​、Cu、糖）。均匀混合物＝溶液＝2 种以上物质物理混合，比例可变（盐水、空气、汽水、水和酒精）。
  3. Pure substances split into elements (1 type of atom) and compounds (more than 1 type). Every heterogeneous mixture is a mechanical mixture (gravel, sand and iron filings, trail mix).纯净物分为元素（单质）（1 种原子）和化合物（不止 1 种原子）。所有不均匀的混合物都是机械混合物（碎石、沙和铁屑、什锦坚果）。
  4. In a solution the solute is the substance that gets dissolved (salt) and the solvent does the dissolving (water).溶液里，溶质是被溶解的物质（盐），溶剂是起溶解作用的物质（水）。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes list soil as a homogeneous mixture. Most textbooks call soil heterogeneous (a mechanical mixture), because you can usually see bits of sand, clay and plant matter. On this test, if soil is asked, use the class answer: homogeneous mixture.课堂讲义把土壤列为均匀混合物。多数教材把土壤归为不均匀混合物（机械混合物），因为通常能看到沙粒、黏土和植物残渣。考试如果问到土壤，按讲义答：均匀混合物。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Expect to draw or fill in the classification tree from memory — practise it until you can sketch all eight boxes in under a minute. For any substance, ask the questions in order: phases? fixed composition? types of atom?常考默写或填写分类树——练到一分钟内画出全部八个框。遇到任何物质都按顺序问：几个相？组成固定吗？几种原子？

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做2 methods种解法
Classify as element, compound, solution or mechanical mixture: (a) helium in a balloon (b) table sugar CX12​HX22​OX11​ (c) vinegar (acetic acid dissolved in water) (d) a bowl of granola with raisins分类为元素、化合物、溶液或机械混合物：(a) 气球里的氦气 (b) 白砂糖 CX12​HX22​OX11​ (c) 醋（醋酸溶于水）(d) 一碗加葡萄干的燕麦脆
  1. (a) Helium: one phase, one substance, one type of atom → element.(a) 氦气：一个相、一种物质、一种原子→元素。
  2. (b) Sugar: one phase, fixed formula, three types of atom → compound.(b) 糖：一个相、化学式固定、三种原子→化合物。
  3. (c) Vinegar: one phase, but two substances whose amounts can vary → solution (homogeneous mixture).(c) 醋：一个相，但含两种物质、比例可变→溶液（均匀混合物）。
  4. (d) Granola: you can see oats and raisins → more than one phase → mechanical mixture.(d) 燕麦脆：看得见燕麦和葡萄干→不止一个相→机械混合物。
  1. He has a formula with one symbol → element. CX12​HX22​OX11​ has one formula, three symbols → compound.He 有化学式、只有一种符号→元素。CX12​HX22​OX11​ 有单一化学式、三种符号→化合物。
  2. Vinegar and granola have no single formula → mixtures. Then the phase test: vinegar uniform → solution; granola has parts → mechanical.醋和燕麦脆没有单一化学式→混合物。再看相：醋均匀→溶液；燕麦脆有不同部分→机械混合物。
Answer答案
(a) element (b) compound (c) solution (d) mechanical mixture(a) 元素 (b) 化合物 (c) 溶液 (d) 机械混合物

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
Salt water and pure water both look clear and uniform. (a) Where does each go on the tree? (b) How are they different? (c) In salt water, name the solute and the solvent.盐水和纯水看起来都澄清均匀。(a) 它们各在分类树的哪里？(b) 两者有什么不同？(c) 盐水中溶质和溶剂分别是什么？
  1. Both are one phase → both under HOMOGENEOUS SUBSTANCES.两者都是一个相→都在「均匀物质」下面。
  2. Pure water is always HX2​O — fixed composition → pure substance → compound.纯水永远是 HX2​O——组成固定→纯净物→化合物。
  3. Salt water can be a little salty or very salty — variable composition → homogeneous mixture → solution.盐水可以淡也可以很咸——组成可变→均匀混合物→溶液。
  1. Water: 1 substance → pure. Salt water: 2 substances mixed → mixture; one phase → solution.水：1 种物质→纯净物。盐水：2 种物质混合→混合物；一个相→溶液。
  2. Salt is dissolved → solute. Water does the dissolving → solvent.盐被溶解→溶质。水起溶解作用→溶剂。
Answer答案
(a) water: compound; salt water: solution (b) fixed vs variable composition (c) solute salt, solvent water(a) 水：化合物；盐水：溶液 (b) 组成固定 vs 组成可变 (c) 溶质是盐，溶剂是水

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Calling every clear liquid "pure". Salt water and soda pop look uniform but are solutions (mixtures).把澄清的液体都当纯净物。盐水、汽水看起来均匀，但是溶液（混合物）。
  • Putting compounds under mixtures because they contain 2+ elements. A compound has a fixed formula — it is a pure substance.因为化合物含 2 种以上元素就把它归为混合物。化合物化学式固定——是纯净物。
  • Mixing up solute and solvent: the solvent is usually the larger amount that does the dissolving (water).搞反溶质和溶剂：溶剂通常是量较多、起溶解作用的那一种（水）。

Where did the white solid come from?白色固体从哪里来？
A solution. It is one phase (clear, uniform), so it is homogeneous — but heating left a white solid, so it contained at least two substances: a dissolved solid (solute) in a liquid (solvent). Two substances with variable amounts = homogeneous mixture = solution.溶液。它是一个相（澄清均匀），所以是均匀物质——但加热后留下白色固体，说明它至少含两种物质：溶解的固体（溶质）和液体（溶剂）。两种物质、比例可变＝均匀混合物＝溶液。
  1. Clear and uniform → one phase → homogeneous (pure substance OR solution).澄清均匀→一个相→均匀物质（纯净物或溶液）。
  2. A pure liquid would leave nothing behind. A white solid remained → a dissolved solute was present.纯液体蒸干后什么也不会留下。留下了白色固体→其中溶有溶质。
