📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Diagonal = temperature changing (one phase). Horizontal = phase changing (two phases), T constant.斜线＝温度变（一个相）。水平线＝相变（两个相），温度不变。
  • Water: 1 ice warms · 2 melts at 0 °C · 3 liquid warms · 4 boils at 100 °C · 5 steam warms.水：1 冰升温 · 2 0 °C 熔化 · 3 液体升温 · 4 100 °C 沸腾 · 5 水蒸气升温。
  • Flat because energy breaks attractions or separates particles, not speeding them up.平台是因为能量用来打破吸引或分开粒子，而不是让粒子加速。

💡 The burner under a pot of ice water is on full, yet the thermometer is stuck at 0 °C for minutes — where is all that heat going?一锅冰水下面的火开到最大，温度计却好几分钟都停在 0 °C——那么多热量跑到哪里去了？

  1. Axes: temperature goes up the y-axis; heat added (energy entering the system) goes along the x-axis. Very important rule: a diagonal line means the temperature is changing; a horizontal line means the phase is changing and the temperature is constant.坐标轴：纵轴是温度；横轴是加入的热量（进入体系的能量）。最重要的规则：斜线表示温度在变；水平线表示相在变，温度不变。
  2. Water from −20 °C to 120 °C: (1) ice warms to 0 °C · (2) melting at 0 °C · (3) liquid water warms 0 → 100 °C · (4) boiling at 100 °C · (5) steam warms above 100 °C.水从 −20 °C 加热到 120 °C：(1) 冰升温到 0 °C · (2) 0 °C 熔化 · (3) 液态水从 0 升到 100 °C · (4) 100 °C 沸腾 · (5) 水蒸气在 100 °C 以上升温。
  3. On the flat parts, two phases are present (solid + liquid at 0 °C; liquid + gas at 100 °C). The added energy breaks the attractions holding particles together (melting) or separates particles into a gas (boiling) — it does not make them move faster, so the temperature stays the same.水平段上同时存在两个相（0 °C 时固＋液；100 °C 时液＋气）。加入的能量用来打破粒子间的吸引（熔化）或把粒子分开成气体（沸腾）——不是让粒子跑得更快，所以温度不变。
  4. A cooling curve is the same graph read backwards: steam cools → condenses at 100 °C → liquid cools → freezes at 0 °C → ice cools. On the flat parts, energy is released.冷却曲线就是把同一张图倒过来读：水蒸气降温 → 100 °C 液化 → 液态水降温 → 0 °C 凝固 → 冰降温。水平段上放出能量。

🧮 Key formulas关键公式

diagonal⇒temperature changes;horizontal⇒phase changes
The one rule for reading any heating or cooling curve读任何加热或冷却曲线的唯一规则：斜线 → 温度变；水平线 → 相变
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes put "heat added" on the x-axis. Many textbooks put "time" there instead (with heat added at a steady rate) — the shape and the five segments are exactly the same, so read either graph the same way.课堂笔记的横轴是「加入的热量」。很多教科书横轴写的是「时间」（假设匀速加热）——曲线形状和五段完全一样，两种图读法相同。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Sketch the curve with 0 °C and 100 °C marked and number the segments 1–5 before answering. For "why constant?" always write: energy breaks attractions / separates particles instead of increasing their speed.答题前先画曲线，标出 0 °C 和 100 °C，并给五段编号。问「为什么温度不变」时一律写：能量用来打破吸引／分开粒子，而不是提高粒子速度。

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做
For each segment 1–5 of the heating curve of water, state the phase(s) present and whether the temperature is changing.对水的加热曲线第 1–5 段，分别写出存在的相，以及温度是否在变化。
  1. 1 (diagonal): solid only; temperature rising −20 → 0 °C.1（斜线）：只有固体；温度从 −20 升到 0 °C。
  2. 2 (horizontal): solid + liquid; constant at 0 °C (melting).2（水平）：固体＋液体；温度恒定在 0 °C（熔化）。
  3. 3 (diagonal): liquid only; rising 0 → 100 °C.3（斜线）：只有液体；从 0 升到 100 °C。
  4. 4 (horizontal): liquid + gas; constant at 100 °C (boiling).4（水平）：液体＋气体；恒定在 100 °C（沸腾）。
  5. 5 (diagonal): gas only; rising above 100 °C.5（斜线）：只有气体；温度升到 100 °C 以上。

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
Ice is melting in a beaker on a hot plate. Heat is still flowing in, but the thermometer stays at 0 °C. Explain why.烧杯里的冰在电热板上熔化，热量一直在进入，但温度计停在 0 °C。解释原因。
  1. During melting, the added heat is used to break the attractions holding the particles in the solid arrangement.熔化时，加入的热量用来打破把粒子固定在固体排列中的吸引力。
  2. None of it is left over to make the particles move faster, so the temperature stays at 0 °C until all the ice has melted.没有多余的能量让粒子运动得更快，所以在冰全部熔化之前，温度一直是 0 °C。
  1. Temperature rises only when particles speed up (more kinetic energy).只有粒子加速（动能增大）时，温度才会升高。
  2. On the flat part the particles are breaking free and starting to slide past each other, but their average speed does not change → temperature constant. Both solid and liquid are present.在水平段，粒子正在挣脱、开始彼此滑动，但平均速度不变 → 温度不变。此时固体和液体同时存在。
Answer答案
The heat breaks attractions between particles instead of speeding them up热量用来打破粒子之间的吸引，而不是让它们加速

Example 3例 3L4 Solve会想2 methods种解法
Steam at 120 °C is cooled steadily until it becomes ice at −20 °C. Describe the cooling curve segment by segment: what is happening, the temperature, and the phase(s) present.120 °C 的水蒸气被匀速冷却，直到变成 −20 °C 的冰。逐段描述冷却曲线：发生了什么、温度多少、存在哪些相。
  1. Steam cools 120 → 100 °C: gas only, temperature falling.水蒸气从 120 降到 100 °C：只有气体，温度下降。
  2. Condensation at 100 °C: gas + liquid, temperature constant, energy released.100 °C 液化：气体＋液体，温度不变，放出能量。
  3. Liquid water cools 100 → 0 °C: liquid only, temperature falling.液态水从 100 降到 0 °C：只有液体，温度下降。
  4. Freezing at 0 °C: liquid + solid, temperature constant, energy released. Then ice cools 0 → −20 °C: solid only.0 °C 凝固：液体＋固体，温度不变，放出能量。然后冰从 0 降到 −20 °C：只有固体。
  1. On the triangle, gas → liquid is condensation and liquid → solid is freezing.在三角图上，气 → 液是液化，液 → 固是凝固。
  2. For water these happen at the same temperatures as boiling and melting: 100 °C and 0 °C. Between them the line slopes down (temperature falling).对水来说，它们和沸腾、熔化发生在同样的温度：100 °C 和 0 °C。中间各段是向下的斜线（温度下降）。
Answer答案
gas cools → condenses at 100 °C → liquid cools → freezes at 0 °C → ice cools气体降温 → 100 °C 液化（凝结） → 液体降温 → 0 °C 凝固 → 冰降温

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Thinking the temperature keeps rising while ice melts because "heat is going in". On a flat part, the heat goes into breaking attractions, not into raising temperature.以为冰熔化时「热量在进」所以温度还在升。水平段上，热量用来打破吸引力，不是用来升温。
  • Saying only one phase is present on a flat part. During melting both solid and liquid are there; during boiling both liquid and gas.说水平段上只有一个相。熔化时固体和液体同时存在；沸腾时液体和气体同时存在。
  • Calling segment 1 "melting" because the temperature is heading toward 0 °C. Segment 1 is solid ice warming up — no phase change yet.因为温度正朝 0 °C 走，就把第 1 段说成熔化。第 1 段是固态冰在升温——还没有相变。

Why does the temperature stay constant while water boils, even though the burner is still on?为什么水沸腾时，即使火一直开着，温度也保持不变？
Boiling is a physical change — the molecules stay HX2​O.沸腾是物理变化——分子仍是 HX2​O。
The heat separates particles into the gas phase instead of speeding them up.热量用于把粒子分开成气体，而不是让它们加速。
  1. During boiling, energy is used to pull particles away from their neighbours into the gas phase, not to increase their average speed, so the temperature stays at 100 °C.沸腾时，能量用来把粒子从邻近粒子中拉出、进入气相，而不是提高平均速度，所以温度保持在 100 °C。

Spinning or shaking in place does not let a particle escape.原地转或原地抖都不能让粒子逃出去。
translational平动动能
