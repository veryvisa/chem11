📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Physical property: observe or measure, substance unchanged (colour, density, mp, bp, hardness).物理性质：观察或测量，物质不变（颜色、密度、熔点、沸点、硬度）。
  • Chemical property: how it reacts to form a new substance (rusts, burns, reacts with acid, tarnishes).化学性质：能怎样反应生成新物质（生锈、燃烧、与酸反应、变暗）。
  • Matter has mass and takes up space; energy (heat, light) is not matter.物质有质量、占空间；能量（热、光）不是物质。

💡 One bottle label says "colourless liquid" and also "highly flammable" — one line tells you what the liquid is like, the other tells you what it can turn into.同一个瓶子的标签上写着「无色液体」，又写着「高度易燃」——一句告诉你它长什么样，另一句告诉你它能变成什么。

  1. Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space (solid, liquid, gas). Energy such as heat or light is NOT matter: it has no mass and takes up no space.物质是有质量、占有空间的一切东西（固体、液体、气体）。热、光这类能量不是物质：它们没有质量，也不占空间。
  2. A physical property is something you can observe or measure WITHOUT changing the substance: colour, density, melting point, boiling point, hardness, lustre, viscosity, state at room temperature.物理性质：不改变物质本身就能观察或测量到的性质，例如颜色、密度、熔点、沸点、硬度、光泽、黏度、室温下的状态。
  3. A chemical property describes how a substance can REACT to form a new substance: ability to rust, ability to burn (flammable), reacts with acid or water, tarnishes. To see it, a chemical change has to happen.化学性质：描述物质能怎样发生反应、生成新物质，例如会生锈、能燃烧（易燃）、与酸或水反应、会失去光泽（变暗）。要看到它，就必须发生化学变化。
  4. Quick test: "Can I check this just by looking or measuring, and still have the same stuff?" Yes → physical. Words like rusts, burns, flammable, reacts, tarnishes → chemical.快速判断：「只靠看或量就能知道，而且东西还是原来的东西吗？」是 → 物理性质。出现生锈、燃烧、易燃、反应、变暗（失去光泽）等词 → 化学性质。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
These come as quick "P or C" lists. Underline the verb: is/has/boils at/is hard → P; rusts/burns/reacts/tarnishes/flammable → C. Answer with the full word.这类题常以「P 还是 C」的列表出现。圈出动词：是／有／在……沸腾／坚硬 → 物理；生锈／燃烧／反应／变暗／易燃 → 化学。答题时写完整的词。

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做
Classify each as a physical or chemical property: (a) Ethanol boils at 78 °C. (b) Gasoline is flammable. (c) Copper has a reddish-brown colour.判断下列各项是物理性质还是化学性质：(a) 乙醇在 78 °C 沸腾。(b) 汽油易燃。(c) 铜呈红棕色。
  1. (a) Boiling point is measured; the ethanol is still ethanol (just a gas) → physical.(a) 沸点是测量出来的，乙醇还是乙醇（只是变成气体）→ 物理性质。
  2. (b) Flammable means it CAN burn and become new substances (carbon dioxide, water) → chemical.(b) 易燃表示它能燃烧，变成新物质（二氧化碳、水）→ 化学性质。
  3. (c) Colour is seen by looking → physical.(c) 颜色用眼睛看就知道 → 物理性质。
Answer答案
(a) physical, (b) chemical, (c) physical(a) 物理性质，(b) 化学性质，(c) 物理性质

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
A student writes: "Sodium is a soft, shiny metal that reacts violently with water." Pick out every property and classify it.某学生写道：「钠是一种柔软、有光泽的金属，遇水会剧烈反应。」找出其中每一个性质并分类。
  1. Soft → hardness (how easily it is scratched) → physical.柔软 → 硬度（是否容易被划伤）→ 物理性质。
  2. Shiny → lustre → physical.有光泽 → 光泽 → 物理性质。
  3. Reacts violently with water → new substances form → chemical.遇水剧烈反应 → 生成新物质 → 化学性质。
  1. Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity are all physical properties — you test them without making a new substance.硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度都是物理性质——检测时不会生成新物质。
  2. Anything with "reacts" is a chemical property. So: soft (P), shiny (P), reacts with water (C).凡是「会反应」的都是化学性质。所以：柔软（物理）、有光泽（物理）、遇水反应（化学）。
Answer答案
soft, shiny: physical; reacts with water: chemical软、有光泽：物理性质；与水反应：化学性质

Example 3例 3L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
Iron rusts when exposed to oxygen, and silver tarnishes in air. A friend says: "These are not properties, they are things that happen, so they cannot be classified." Explain what kind of property each is and why.铁接触氧气会生锈，银在空气中会变暗。同学说：「这些是发生的事，不是性质，没法分类。」请说明它们各属于哪类性质，为什么。
  1. Rust is a new substance made from iron and oxygen; tarnish is a new dark substance on the silver surface.铁锈是铁和氧气生成的新物质；银变暗是表面生成了新的黑色物质。
  2. A property that describes how a substance reacts to form a new substance is a chemical property.描述物质如何反应、生成新物质的性质，就是化学性质。
  1. The property is the ability (iron CAN rust). The rusting itself, when it happens, is a chemical change.性质是能力（铁「会」生锈）；真正发生生锈的过程，是化学变化。
  2. So on a "property" question, "iron rusts in oxygen" → chemical property; on a "change" question, "the nail rusted" → chemical change.所以在「性质」题里，「铁在氧气中会生锈」→ 化学性质；在「变化」题里，「钉子生锈了」→ 化学变化。
Answer答案
Both are chemical properties: each describes reacting to form a new substance两者都是化学性质：都描述反应生成新物质的能力

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Calling colour a chemical property because colour can change in a reaction. Colour itself is seen by looking → physical; only "changes colour by reacting" points to chemical.因为反应时颜色会变，就把颜色当成化学性质。颜色本身用眼看就知道 → 物理性质；只有「通过反应变色」才指向化学。
  • Thinking "flammable" is physical because nothing is burning right now. A chemical property is the ABILITY to react; it does not have to be happening.因为现在没在烧，就认为「易燃」是物理性质。化学性质是发生反应的「能力」，不必正在发生。
  • Counting heat or light as matter. They are energy: no mass, no space taken up.把热或光当成物质。它们是能量：没有质量，也不占空间。

Two unlabelled clear liquids are on the lab bench. Suggest one physical property and one chemical property you could test to tell them apart, and say which test leaves the sample unchanged.实验台上有两瓶无标签的透明液体。各举一个可以用来区分它们的物理性质和化学性质，并说明哪种测试不会改变样品。
Think of the ethanol example from class.想想课上乙醇的例子。
e.g. physical: boiling point or density (sample unchanged); chemical: flammability (sample becomes new substances).例如物理：沸点或密度（样品不变）；化学：可燃性（样品变成新物质）。
  1. Physical property test: measure each boiling point or density. The liquid is still the same substance afterwards.物理性质测试：测各自的沸点或密度，之后液体还是原来的物质。
  2. Chemical property test: see if a small drop burns. Burning turns it into new substances, so that part of the sample is gone.化学性质测试：看一小滴能否燃烧。燃烧会把它变成新物质，那部分样品就没了。
