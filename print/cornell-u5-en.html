The six phase changes六种相变

Remember记住
  • s→l melting · l→s freezing · l→g evaporation · g→l condensation固→液 熔化 · 液→固 凝固 · 液→气 汽化 · 气→液 液化

Watch out当心
  • Frost = freezing ✗ → frost = deposition (vapour → ice) ✓霜＝凝固 ✗ → 霜＝凝华（水蒸气 → 冰）✓

Heating curve of water水的加热曲线

Formulas公式
diagonal⇒temperature changes;horizontal⇒phase changes — The one rule for reading any heating or cooling curve读任何加热或冷却曲线的唯一规则：斜线 → 温度变；水平线 → 相变
Remember记住
  • Diagonal = temperature changing (one phase). Horizontal = phase changing (two phases), T constant.斜线＝温度变（一个相）。水平线＝相变（两个相），温度不变。
  • Water: 1 ice warms · 2 melts at 0 °C · 3 liquid warms · 4 boils at 100 °C · 5 steam warms.水：1 冰升温 · 2 0 °C 熔化 · 3 液体升温 · 4 100 °C 沸腾 · 5 水蒸气升温。
Watch out当心
  • Heat added → temperature must rise ✗ → not on the flat parts ✓加热 → 温度一定升 ✗ → 水平段上不升 ✓
  • Flat part = one phase ✗ → two phases together ✓水平段＝一个相 ✗ → 两个相共存 ✓

Kinetic energy: rotational, vibrational, translational动能：转动、振动、平动

Remember记住
  • Kinetic energy = energy of motion; temperature up → faster → more kinetic energy.动能＝运动的能量；温度升 → 更快 → 动能更大。
  • Rotational spins (bonds same) · vibrational stretches/bends in place · translational moves place to place.转动：旋转（键不变）· 振动：原地伸缩弯曲 · 平动：从一处移到另一处。
Watch out当心
  • Vibrating molecule moves across the box ✗ → vibration is in place; moving across is translational ✓振动的分子会穿过盒子 ✗ → 振动在原地；穿过盒子是平动 ✓
