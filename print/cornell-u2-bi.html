Matter and its states物质与物质的状态

Remember记住
  • Matter = has mass + takes up space. 4 states: solid, liquid, gas, plasma.物质＝有质量＋占空间。4 种状态：固、液、气、等离子体。
  • Solid: definite shape and volume. Liquid: indefinite shape, definite volume. Gas: both indefinite.固体：形状体积都固定。液体：形状不定、体积固定。气体：都不固定。
Watch out当心
  • Liquid has indefinite volume → it is the shape that changes; volume is definite.液体体积不固定 → 变的是形状，体积固定。

Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity, diffusion, vapour硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度、扩散、蒸气

Remember记住
  • Malleable = sheets; ductile = wires; hardness = resists scratching; lustre = shiny vs dull.可锻性＝成片；延性＝成丝；硬度＝抗刮；光泽＝亮或暗。
  • Viscosity = resistance to flow (honey high). Diffusion = high → low concentration to equilibrium.黏度＝抗流动（蜂蜜高）。扩散＝高→低浓度直到平衡。
Watch out当心
  • Malleable = wire → no: malleable = sheet, ductile = wire.可锻性＝成丝 → 错：可锻性＝成片，延性＝成丝。
  • High viscosity = runny → high viscosity = thick, slow.黏度高＝稀 → 黏度高＝稠、流得慢。

System vs phase体系与相

Remember记住
  • System = what you are studying. Phase = a uniform part of the system.体系＝研究对象。相＝体系中均一的部分。
  • Different states or layers = different phases. Gas mixtures = 1 phase. Solutions = 1 phase.不同状态或分层＝不同的相。气体混合物＝1 相。溶液＝1 相。
Watch out当心
  • Two gases = 2 phases → gases always mix into 1 phase.两种气体＝2 相 → 气体总是混成 1 相。
  • Salt water = 2 phases (two substances) → 1 phase.盐水＝2 相（两种物质）→ 1 相。
