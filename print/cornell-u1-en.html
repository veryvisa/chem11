Qualitative, quantitative, observation & interpretation定性、定量、观察与解释

Remember记住
  • Qualitative = senses, no numbers. Quantitative = measured or counted numbers.定性＝感官，无数字。定量＝测量或计数的数字。
  • Observation = qualitative, sensed directly. Interpretation = what it means, not directly seen.观察＝定性、直接感知。解释＝它意味着什么，看不见。
Watch out当心
  • "4 beakers" is qualitative → it is a count, so quantitative.「4 个烧杯」是定性 → 计数，是定量。
  • "A gas was produced" is an observation → interpretation; "bubbles formed" is the observation.「产生了气体」是观察 → 是解释；「出现气泡」才是观察。

Data, experiment, hypothesis, theory & law数据、实验、假设、理论与定律

Remember记住
  • Data = numbers from an experiment; it supports or does not support a hypothesis, never proves it.数据＝实验得到的数字；只支持或不支持假设，不能证明。
  • Hypothesis = one unproven, testable guess ("If…, then…"). Experiment = test that gives a result.假设＝单一、未证实、可检验的猜测（如果……那么……）。实验＝得出结果的测试。
Watch out当心
  • "The data proves the hypothesis" → "the data supports the hypothesis".「数据证明了假设」→「数据支持了假设」。
  • "A theory becomes a law when proven" → they never convert; why vs what.「理论被证明后变成定律」→ 两者不会转化；为什么 vs 是什么。
