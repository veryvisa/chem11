📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Qualitative = senses, no numbers. Quantitative = measured or counted numbers.定性＝感官，无数字。定量＝测量或计数的数字。
  • Observation = qualitative, sensed directly. Interpretation = what it means, not directly seen.观察＝定性、直接感知。解释＝它意味着什么，看不见。
  • Description = properties in words and numbers, so it can be both.描述＝用文字和数字列性质，可以两者兼有。

💡 Your lab partner writes "the reaction is exothermic" under Observations. On this test that line loses marks — it is an interpretation. Knowing which box each sentence belongs in is free marks.实验搭档在「观察」一栏写了「这个反应是放热反应」。考试里这句会被扣分——它是解释，不是观察。分清每句话该放哪一栏，就是白拿的分。

  1. Qualitative = no numbers, described with your senses (colour, smell, feel, sound, "bubbly", "hot"). Quantitative = a number from a measurement or a count (46.5 mL, 21.23 mg, 4 beakers).定性＝没有数字，用感官描述（颜色、气味、手感、声音、「冒泡」「很烫」）。定量＝来自测量或计数的数字（46.5 mL、21.23 mg、4 个烧杯）。
  2. An observation is information you get directly with your senses. In this class an observation is always qualitative.观察＝直接用感官得到的信息。本课规定：观察一定是定性的。
  3. An interpretation explains what an observation means. It is based on evidence but is an idea you cannot directly see ("a gas was produced, so a reaction happened").解释＝说明观察到的现象意味着什么。它有证据支撑，但本身是一个看不见的想法（「产生了气体，所以发生了反应」）。
  4. A description lists properties using words and/or numbers, so it can be both qualitative and quantitative ("clear, colourless, 50 mL").描述＝用文字和／或数字列出性质，所以可以同时是定性和定量的（「澄清、无色、50 mL」）。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes say an observation must be qualitative (measurements count as quantitative data, not observations). Many textbooks call measurements "quantitative observations". On this test use the class version: observation = qualitative.课堂讲义规定观察必须是定性的（测量得到的数字算定量数据，不算观察）。很多教材把测量叫作「定量观察」。考试按讲义：观察＝定性。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Expect a two-column table: write Q/N or O/I next to each line. Circle any number first — a number means quantitative and rules out "observation".常考两栏表格：在每行旁写定性／定量或观察／解释。先圈出数字——有数字就是定量，而且不可能是「观察」。

Example 1例 1L1 Know知道
Qualitative or quantitative? (a) The test tube holds 12 mL of liquid. (b) The powder smells like vinegar. (c) There are 3 thermometers on the bench. (d) Magnesium is a shiny grey metal.定性还是定量？(a) 试管里有 12 mL 液体。(b) 这种粉末闻起来像醋。(c) 实验台上有 3 支温度计。(d) 镁是一种有光泽的灰色金属。
  1. (a) 12 mL is a measurement → quantitative.(a) 12 mL 是测量值→定量。
  2. (b) Smell is a sense, no number → qualitative.(b) 气味靠感官，没有数字→定性。
  3. (c) "3 thermometers" is a count → quantitative (counting is numerical).(c) 「3 支温度计」是计数→定量（计数也是数字信息）。
  4. (d) Shiny and grey are seen, no number → qualitative.(d) 有光泽、灰色是看出来的，没有数字→定性。
Answer答案
(a) quantitative, (b) qualitative, (c) quantitative, (d) qualitative(a) 定量，(b) 定性，(c) 定量，(d) 定性

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
A student drops a tablet into water and writes: "Bubbles formed and the beaker felt warm, so the reaction released heat and is exothermic." Split the sentence into observation(s) and interpretation(s).学生把一片药片放进水里，写道：「产生了气泡，烧杯摸起来变热，所以反应放出了热量，是放热反应。」把这句话拆成观察和解释。
  1. "Bubbles formed" — seen → observation.「产生了气泡」——看得到→观察。
  2. "The beaker felt warm" / "heat was released" — felt by touch → observation (the class notes mark "heat was released" as an observation).「烧杯变热」／「放出了热量」——摸得到→观察（讲义把「放出了热量」标为观察）。
  3. "The reaction is exothermic" — a chemistry idea you cannot see; it explains the warmth → interpretation.「这是放热反应」——看不见的化学概念，用来解释变热→解释。
  1. The word "so" splits evidence from conclusion: before it = what was sensed, after it = what it means.「所以」把证据和结论分开：前面是感觉到的，后面是它的含义。
  2. Careful: "released heat" sits after "so" but is still something you feel. Only the label "exothermic" is the pure interpretation. Always finish with the senses test.注意：「放出热量」虽在「所以」后面，但仍是摸得到的。只有「放热反应」这个结论才是纯粹的解释。最后一定要用感官检验确认。
Answer答案
Observations: bubbles formed, warm (heat released). Interpretation: exothermic.观察：产生气泡、变热（放出热量）。解释：放热反应。
🧭 What this example trains这道题练什么
🎯 Goal考查目标
Separate what was sensed from what was concluded inside one sentence.在同一句话里分开「感觉到的」和「推断出的」。
📚 Knowledge used用到的知识
Qualitative, quantitative, observation & interpretation定性、定量、观察与解释
💪 Abilities锻炼的能力
📘 Concepts概念理解🔍 Checking & reasoning检验与推理
💡 Key idea解题思路
Ask of each part: could I see, hear, smell or feel this directly? If it needs a reason ("so…", "because…"), it is interpretation.对每一部分问：我能直接看到、听到、闻到、摸到吗？需要「所以」「因为」才说得出的就是解释。
🔀 Other ways to think不同的思考方式
  • Senses test: directly sensed → observation.感官检验：直接感知→观察。
  • Signal-word test: the part after "so" or "because" is usually the interpretation.信号词检验：「所以」「因为」后面通常是解释。

Is "The solid is white, powdery and has a mass of 5.0 g" qualitative, quantitative, or both? What kind of statement is it?「这种固体是白色粉末状，质量为 5.0 g」是定性、定量还是两者都有？它属于哪一类说法？
  1. White and powdery → qualitative. 5.0 g → quantitative.白色、粉末状→定性。5.0 g→定量。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Calling a count qualitative: "There are 4 beakers" has a number, so it is quantitative.把计数当成定性：「有 4 个烧杯」含数字，是定量。
  • Writing a conclusion under Observations: "a gas was produced" or "a chemical reaction occurred" are interpretations; "bubbles formed" is the observation.把结论写进「观察」：「产生了气体」「发生了化学反应」是解释；「出现气泡」才是观察。
  • Calling a measurement an observation: in this class observations are qualitative only; a reading like 46.5 mL is quantitative information.把测量值当观察：本课观察只能是定性的；46.5 mL 这样的读数是定量信息。
