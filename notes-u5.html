The six phase changes六种相变

  • There are three states (solid, liquid, gas) and six arrows between them. Each pair goes both ways: melting ⇄ freezing, evaporation ⇄ condensation, sublimation ⇄ deposition.三种状态（固、液、气）之间有六个箭头，每两种状态之间来回各一个：熔化 ⇄ 凝固，汽化 ⇄ 液化，升华 ⇄ 凝华。
  • Solid → liquid melting; liquid → solid freezing; liquid → gas evaporation; gas → liquid condensation; solid → gas sublimation (skips liquid); gas → solid deposition (skips liquid).固 → 液 熔化；液 → 固 凝固；液 → 气 汽化；气 → 液 液化（凝结）；固 → 气 升华（跳过液态）；气 → 固 凝华（跳过液态）。
  • Every phase change is a PHYSICAL change: the substance stays the same, e.g. HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l). Read the state symbols (s), (l), (g) to name the change.每一种相变都是物理变化：物质不变，例如 HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l)。看状态符号 (s)、(l)、(g) 就能说出是哪种相变。
  • Toward gas (melting, evaporation, sublimation) the substance absorbs energy to pull particles apart. Away from gas (freezing, condensation, deposition) it releases energy.朝气态方向（熔化、汽化、升华）要吸收能量，把粒子拉开；离开气态方向（凝固、液化、凝华）会放出能量。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Mixing up sublimation and deposition. Sublimation starts as a SOLID (dry ice); deposition starts as a GAS (frost).把升华和凝华搞反。升华从固体开始（干冰）；凝华从气体开始（霜）。
  • Calling gas → liquid "evaporation" or liquid → gas "condensation". Condensation makes drops (liquid); evaporation makes gas.把气 → 液说成汽化，或把液 → 气说成液化。液化产生水滴（液体）；汽化产生气体。
  • Calling frost "freezing". Freezing starts from liquid; frost forms straight from water vapour → deposition.把结霜说成凝固。凝固从液体开始；霜是水蒸气直接变成的 → 凝华。

🎯 Draw the triangle (gas on top, solid bottom-left, liquid bottom-right) in the margin before you start — then every naming question is just "follow the arrow".考试开始先在页边画出三角图（气在上，固在左下，液在右下）——之后所有命名题都只是「沿箭头读」。

Heating curve of water水的加热曲线

  • Axes: temperature goes up the y-axis; heat added (energy entering the system) goes along the x-axis. Very important rule: a diagonal line means the temperature is changing; a horizontal line means the phase is changing and the temperature is constant.坐标轴：纵轴是温度；横轴是加入的热量（进入体系的能量）。最重要的规则：斜线表示温度在变；水平线表示相在变，温度不变。
  • Water from −20 °C to 120 °C: (1) ice warms to 0 °C · (2) melting at 0 °C · (3) liquid water warms 0 → 100 °C · (4) boiling at 100 °C · (5) steam warms above 100 °C.水从 −20 °C 加热到 120 °C：(1) 冰升温到 0 °C · (2) 0 °C 熔化 · (3) 液态水从 0 升到 100 °C · (4) 100 °C 沸腾 · (5) 水蒸气在 100 °C 以上升温。
  • On the flat parts, two phases are present (solid + liquid at 0 °C; liquid + gas at 100 °C). The added energy breaks the attractions holding particles together (melting) or separates particles into a gas (boiling) — it does not make them move faster, so the temperature stays the same.水平段上同时存在两个相（0 °C 时固＋液；100 °C 时液＋气）。加入的能量用来打破粒子间的吸引（熔化）或把粒子分开成气体（沸腾）——不是让粒子跑得更快，所以温度不变。
  • A cooling curve is the same graph read backwards: steam cools → condenses at 100 °C → liquid cools → freezes at 0 °C → ice cools. On the flat parts, energy is released.冷却曲线就是把同一张图倒过来读：水蒸气降温 → 100 °C 液化 → 液态水降温 → 0 °C 凝固 → 冰降温。水平段上放出能量。
diagonal⇒temperature changes;horizontal⇒phase changes
The one rule for reading any heating or cooling curve读任何加热或冷却曲线的唯一规则：斜线 → 温度变；水平线 → 相变
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Thinking the temperature keeps rising while ice melts because "heat is going in". On a flat part, the heat goes into breaking attractions, not into raising temperature.以为冰熔化时「热量在进」所以温度还在升。水平段上，热量用来打破吸引力，不是用来升温。
  • Saying only one phase is present on a flat part. During melting both solid and liquid are there; during boiling both liquid and gas.说水平段上只有一个相。熔化时固体和液体同时存在；沸腾时液体和气体同时存在。
  • Calling segment 1 "melting" because the temperature is heading toward 0 °C. Segment 1 is solid ice warming up — no phase change yet.因为温度正朝 0 °C 走，就把第 1 段说成熔化。第 1 段是固态冰在升温——还没有相变。

🎯 Sketch the curve with 0 °C and 100 °C marked and number the segments 1–5 before answering. For "why constant?" always write: energy breaks attractions / separates particles instead of increasing their speed.答题前先画曲线，标出 0 °C 和 100 °C，并给五段编号。问「为什么温度不变」时一律写：能量用来打破吸引／分开粒子，而不是提高粒子速度。

Kinetic energy: rotational, vibrational, translational动能：转动、振动、平动

  • Kinetic energy is the energy particles have because they are moving. Particles are always moving; the faster they move, the more kinetic energy they have. Temperature up → particles faster → kinetic energy up.动能是粒子因运动而具有的能量。粒子一直在运动；运动越快，动能越大。温度升高 → 粒子更快 → 动能增大。
  • Rotational: the molecule spins in place; bond lengths and angles stay the same. Vibrational: atoms shake (bonds stretch and bend); bond lengths and angles change, but the molecule stays in the same place.转动：分子原地旋转，键长和键角不变。振动：原子来回抖动（键伸缩、弯曲），键长和键角改变，但分子留在原处。
  • Translational: the whole molecule moves from one place to another (travels in a straight line until it hits something); bond lengths and angles do not change.平动：整个分子从一处移到另一处（沿直线运动，直到撞上东西），键长和键角不变。
  • Translational energy matters most for phase changes: particles need enough freedom to pull away from each other. Melting and boiling happen when translational energy reaches a critical value.对相变最重要的是平动能：粒子需要足够的自由才能彼此拉开。平动能达到临界值时，就发生熔化和沸腾。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Mixing up rotational and vibrational. Rotational: bonds unchanged, just spinning. Vibrational: bonds stretch and bend.把转动和振动搞混。转动：键不变，只是旋转。振动：键伸缩、弯曲。
  • Thinking vibrating moves a molecule across the container. Vibration happens in place; moving across is translational.以为振动会让分子穿过容器。振动是原地发生的；穿过容器是平动。
  • Saying kinetic energy increases during melting or boiling. On the flat parts the energy goes into separating particles, so average kinetic energy (temperature) stays the same.说熔化或沸腾时动能在增加。水平段上能量用来分开粒子，平均动能（温度）不变。

🎯 For any "which motion?" question, ask two things: do the bonds change? does the molecule move to a new place? For "which matters most for phase change?" the answer is always translational.遇到「哪种运动」的题，问两件事：键变不变？分子换不换位置？问「对相变最重要的是哪种」，答案永远是平动。
