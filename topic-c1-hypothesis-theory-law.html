📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Data = numbers from an experiment; it supports or does not support a hypothesis, never proves it.数据＝实验得到的数字；只支持或不支持假设，不能证明。
  • Hypothesis = one unproven, testable guess ("If…, then…"). Experiment = test that gives a result.假设＝单一、未证实、可检验的猜测（如果……那么……）。实验＝得出结果的测试。
  • Theory explains WHY and can change. Law describes WHAT, does not explain, often an equation.理论解释为什么、可修改。定律描述是什么、不解释、常写成等式。

💡 Mass before a reaction: 45.6 g. Mass after: 45.6 g. Is that a hypothesis, data, a theory or a law? The test asks exactly this — and "law" and "theory" are the pair everyone mixes up.反应前质量 45.6 g，反应后 45.6 g。这是假设、数据、理论还是定律？考试就考这个——而「定律」和「理论」是最容易搞混的一对。

  1. Data = quantitative information measured in an experiment (numbers). Data can support or not support a hypothesis — it never proves it.数据＝实验中测得的定量信息（数字）。数据可以支持或不支持假设——但永远不能证明它。
  2. An experiment is a test or procedure done to find a result and answer a question. A hypothesis is a single, unproven, testable educated guess, often written "If …, then …".实验＝为得到结果、回答问题而进行的测试或步骤。假设＝单一的、未经证实的、可检验的有根据的猜测，常写成「如果……那么……」。
  3. A theory explains WHY nature behaves a certain way. It is built from many tested hypotheses and can change with new evidence (e.g. Cell Theory).理论＝解释「为什么」自然会这样。它由许多经过检验的假设建立起来，有新证据时可以修改（如细胞学说）。
  4. A law describes WHAT happens but does not explain why. It is an experimentally established pattern, often written as an equation (e.g. Law of Conservation of Mass).定律＝描述「发生了什么」，不解释原因。它是实验反复确认的规律，常写成等式（如质量守恒定律）。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes define data as quantitative only (numbers from an experiment); many textbooks also allow qualitative data. The notes also say data supports or does not support a hypothesis and does not prove it — on this test write "supports", never "proves".课堂讲义把数据定义为只含定量信息（实验得到的数字）；很多教材也承认定性数据。讲义还强调数据只能支持或不支持假设，不能证明它——考试写「支持」，不要写「证明」。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
For theory vs law, ask one question: does it say WHY? Yes → theory. For data questions, never write "proves" — write "supports" or "does not support".分理论和定律，只问一句：它说了「为什么」吗？说了→理论。数据题永远别写「证明」——写「支持」或「不支持」。

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做
Classify each: (a) "If salt is added to water, then the water will boil at a higher temperature." (b) "Readings every minute: 20 °C, 24 °C, 27 °C." (c) "Matter is made of tiny particles that are always moving, which explains why gases spread out." (d) "Energy is neither created nor destroyed."给每句分类：(a)「如果往水里加盐，那么水的沸点会升高。」(b)「每分钟读数：20 °C、24 °C、27 °C。」(c)「物质由不停运动的微小粒子组成，这解释了气体为什么会扩散。」(d)「能量既不会被创造也不会被消灭。」
  1. (a) "If …, then …", not yet tested → hypothesis.(a) 「如果……那么……」，尚未检验→假设。
  2. (b) Measured numbers → data.(b) 测得的数字→数据。
  3. (c) "which explains why" → theory (this is the kinetic molecular theory).(c) 「这解释了为什么」→理论（就是分子动理论）。
  4. (d) States what always happens, no reason given → law (Law of Conservation of Energy).(d) 陈述总会发生的事、不讲原因→定律（能量守恒定律）。
Answer答案
(a) hypothesis, (b) data, (c) theory, (d) law(a) 假设，(b) 数据，(c) 理论，(d) 定律

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
Mass of reactants before a reaction = 45.6 g; mass of products after = 45.6 g. (a) Is this data? (b) Which law does it match? (c) Does it prove the law? (d) Why is the law not a theory?反应物在反应前质量＝45.6 g，生成物在反应后质量＝45.6 g。(a) 这是数据吗？(b) 符合哪条定律？(c) 能证明这条定律吗？(d) 为什么这条定律不是理论？
  1. (a) Yes — quantitative information determined experimentally = data.(a) 是——实验测得的定量信息＝数据。
  2. (b) Total mass before = total mass after → Law of Conservation of Mass: 45.6 g=45.6 g.(b) 反应前总质量＝反应后总质量→质量守恒定律：45.6 g=45.6 g。
  3. (c) No. Data supports a law or hypothesis; one experiment never proves it.(c) 不能。数据只能支持定律或假设；一次实验永远证明不了。
  4. (d) The law only describes what happens to mass. It does not explain why (atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed — that explanation belongs to atomic theory).(d) 定律只描述质量会怎样，并不解释原因（原子只是重新组合、没有产生或消失——这个解释属于原子理论）。
  1. The numbers say WHAT happened (mass stayed the same). A statement of "what" = law.数字说明了发生了什么（质量不变）。说「是什么」的＝定律。
  2. A "why" answer would need particles and bonds — that would be a theory. So the law and the theory are different kinds of statements; a law never "grows up" into a theory.要回答「为什么」就得讲粒子和化学键——那是理论。所以定律和理论是两种不同的说法，定律不会「升级」成理论。
Answer答案
(a) yes, data (b) Conservation of Mass (c) no, it only supports (d) a law describes, it does not explain(a) 是，数据 (b) 质量守恒定律 (c) 不能，数据只是支持 (d) 定律只描述，不解释

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Writing "the data proves the hypothesis". Data only supports or does not support it.写「数据证明了假设」。数据只能支持或不支持假设。
  • Thinking a theory becomes a law after enough tests. They do different jobs: a theory explains why, a law describes what.以为理论检验够多次就变成定律。两者分工不同：理论解释为什么，定律描述是什么。
  • Calling a hypothesis a theory ("my theory is that sugar dissolves faster in hot water"). One untested guess is a hypothesis.把假设叫作理论（「我的理论是糖在热水里溶得更快」）。一个未检验的猜测只是假设。

A student hypothesises: "If water is warmer, then sugar dissolves faster." Times: 120 s at 20 °C, 75 s at 40 °C, 48 s at 60 °C. Does the data support the hypothesis? Can she say it is proven?学生假设：「如果水更热，糖就溶得更快。」计时：20 °C 时 120 s，40 °C 时 75 s，60 °C 时 48 s。数据支持这个假设吗？她能说假设被证明了吗？
Faster dissolving means less time.溶得快意味着用时少。
Yes, it supports the hypothesis (times fall 120 → 75 → 48 s as water warms). No, it is not proven — data only supports.支持（水越热，时间从 120 → 75 → 48 s 下降）。不能说被证明——数据只能支持。
  1. 120>75>48: warmer water, less time → faster dissolving. Supports.120>75>48：水越热用时越少→溶得越快。支持。
  2. One set of data supports it; it does not prove it.一组数据只能支持，不能证明。
🎯 Goal考查目标
Read data against a hypothesis and use "support", not "prove".用数据检验假设，并用「支持」而不是「证明」。

A friend says: "Cell Theory is just a theory, so it is weaker than a law and will become a law once it is proven." Give two reasons this is wrong, using the class definitions.朋友说：「细胞学说只是理论，比定律弱，等被证明了就会变成定律。」用讲义的定义说出两个错误之处。
What does a theory do that a law does not?理论能做什么定律做不到的事？
(1) A theory is not a weak guess: it is tested, refined and supported by many experiments. (2) A theory never turns into a law: a theory explains why, a law only describes what happens. (Also, science does not "prove" — evidence supports.)(1) 理论不是弱的猜测：它经过检验、修正，有许多实验支持。(2) 理论不会变成定律：理论解释为什么，定律只描述发生什么。（另外，科学不说「证明」——证据只是支持。）
  1. A "just a guess" is a hypothesis; a theory is built from many tested hypotheses.「只是猜测」的是假设；理论由许多经过检验的假设构成。
  2. Cell Theory explains why (cells are the basic unit of life). A law would only describe a pattern, so the two do not convert into each other.细胞学说解释原因（细胞是生命的基本单位）。定律只描述规律，所以两者不会互相转化。

Argue about the nature of theories and laws using definitions.用定义论证理论与定律的本质。

Theories and laws do different jobs; neither turns into the other, and theories are built on many experiments.理论和定律分工不同，不会互相转化；理论建立在大量实验之上。
