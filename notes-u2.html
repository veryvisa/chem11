The whole unit on one page: key ideas, formulas, traps. Read before a test; print with the browser if you like paper.整个单元一页看完：核心概念、公式、陷阱。考前读一遍；喜欢纸质的话直接用浏览器打印。

Matter and its states物质与物质的状态

  • Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space (volume). Light, heat and sound are energy, not matter.物质＝有质量并占据空间（体积）的任何东西。光、热、声是能量，不是物质。
  • Matter has 4 common states: solid, liquid, gas and plasma (very hot, charged gas — lightning, the Sun).物质有 4 种常见状态：固体、液体、气体和等离子体（极热、带电的气体——闪电、太阳）。
  • Solid: definite shape, definite volume, particles packed tightly in place, not compressible. Liquid: indefinite shape (takes the container's shape), definite volume, particles touching but sliding past each other, not compressible.固体：形状固定、体积固定，粒子紧密排列、不能移动位置，不可压缩。液体：形状不固定（随容器）、体积固定，粒子相互接触但能彼此滑动，不可压缩。
  • Gas: indefinite shape and indefinite volume (fills any container), particles far apart and touching only when they collide, compressible.气体：形状和体积都不固定（充满任何容器），粒子相距很远、只在碰撞时接触，可压缩。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Saying a liquid has an indefinite volume because it "changes shape". Shape changes, volume does not: 250 mL stays 250 mL in any glass.因为液体「会变形」就说它体积不固定。变的是形状，体积不变：250 mL 倒进哪个杯子都是 250 mL。
  • Calling light or heat matter. They have no mass and take up no space — they are energy.把光或热当作物质。它们没有质量、不占空间——是能量。
  • Forgetting plasma when asked for the 4 common states.问 4 种常见状态时忘了等离子体。

🎯 Memorise the 3 × 4 table (shape, volume, particle arrangement, compressible) — it is often a fill-in-the-blank. Use the words definite / indefinite exactly.背熟 3 × 4 表（形状、体积、粒子排列、可否压缩）——常考填空。用词要准确：definite（固定）／indefinite（不固定）。

Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity, diffusion, vapour硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度、扩散、蒸气

  • Hardness = how well a solid resists scratching (diamond cannot be scratched; calcite can). Lustre = how much a solid reflects light: shiny (glassy, diamond-like, oily, silky) or dull (baking soda).硬度＝固体抵抗刮划的能力（钻石刮不动，方解石能刮出痕）。光泽＝固体表面反光的程度：有光泽（玻璃光泽、金刚光泽、油脂光泽、丝绢光泽）或暗淡无光（小苏打）。
  • Malleability = can be hammered, rolled or shaped into sheets without breaking (aluminum → foil). Ductility = can be stretched or drawn into a thin wire without breaking (copper → wire).可锻性＝能被锤打、轧制成片而不破裂（铝→铝箔）。延性（可拉成丝）＝能被拉成细丝而不断（铜→铜线）。
  • Viscosity = a fluid's resistance to flow: honey high, water low. Diffusion = particles moving from high to low concentration until evenly spread (equilibrium).黏度＝流体抵抗流动的程度：蜂蜜高，水低。扩散＝粒子从高浓度区移向低浓度区，直到分布均匀（达到平衡）。
  • Vapour (class definition) = gas formed when a substance evaporates above room temperature (25 °C), e.g. steam over boiling water. Vapour pressure = pressure made by that vapour, a number in kPa (water: 3.2 kPa).蒸气（讲义定义）＝物质在高于室温（25 °C）时汽化形成的气体，如沸水上方的水蒸气。蒸气压＝蒸气产生的压强，是一个以 kPa 为单位的数值（水：3.2 kPa）。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Mixing up malleable and ductile. Malleable → sheets (think "mallet" = hammer). Ductile → wires.混淆可锻性和延性。可锻性（malleable）→成片（mallet＝锤子）；延性（ductile）→成丝。
  • Thinking high viscosity means "runny". High viscosity = thick and slow (honey); low viscosity = runny (water).以为黏度高就是「稀」。黏度高＝稠、流得慢（蜂蜜）；黏度低＝稀（水）。
  • Writing diffusion as low → high concentration. It always goes high → low.把扩散写成从低浓度到高浓度。方向永远是高→低。
  • Calling lustre "colour". Lustre is about how light reflects (shiny vs dull), not which colour it is.把光泽当成颜色。光泽说的是反光程度（亮或暗），不是什么颜色。

🎯 These come as "match the term to the example". Anchor each word to one class example: diamond/calcite, aluminum foil, copper wire, quartz/baking soda, honey/water, steam, 3.2 kPa.常考「把术语和例子配对」。每个词记一个讲义例子：钻石／方解石、铝箔、铜线、石英／小苏打、蜂蜜／水、水蒸气、3.2 kPa。

System vs phase体系与相

  • A system is the part of the universe you are studying — just what you are focusing on (water in a beaker, gas in a balloon). Name exactly the things being studied.体系＝你正在研究的那部分宇宙——就是你关注的对象（烧杯里的水、气球里的气体）。把正在研究的东西准确说出来。
  • A phase is a part of a system that is uniform (the same) in composition and properties. Different states or visible layers = different phases.相＝体系中组成和性质都均一（处处相同）的部分。不同的状态或看得见的分层＝不同的相。
  • Counting rule: ice + water = 2; oil + water = 2; salt water = 1 (looks the same everywhere). A mixture of gases is always 1 phase. A visible boundary means more than one phase.数相规则：冰＋水＝2；油＋水＝2；盐水＝1（处处看起来一样）。气体混合物永远是 1 相。看得见分界线就说明不止一个相。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Counting substances instead of phases: salt water has two substances but only 1 phase.数物质而不是数相：盐水有两种物质，但只有 1 个相。
  • Saying two gases make two phases. Gases always mix into 1 phase.说两种气体形成两个相。气体总是混成 1 个相。
  • Naming only part of the system: for "sugar dissolving in hot tea" the system is the sugar AND the hot tea.只写出体系的一部分：「糖在热茶中溶解」的体系是糖和热茶两者。

🎯 For phase counts, list every visibly different part in a column and number them. For "identify the system", copy all the things being studied — the class answers name both parts (e.g. "sugar & hot tea").数相时，把每个看起来不同的部分列成一列并编号。「找体系」题要把被研究的东西全写上——讲义答案都写两部分（如「糖和热茶」）。
