📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Kinetic energy = energy of motion; temperature up → faster → more kinetic energy.动能＝运动的能量；温度升 → 更快 → 动能更大。
  • Rotational spins (bonds same) · vibrational stretches/bends in place · translational moves place to place.转动：旋转（键不变）· 振动：原地伸缩弯曲 · 平动：从一处移到另一处。
  • Translational energy matters most for phase changes: particles must pull away from each other.平动能对相变最重要：粒子必须彼此拉开。

💡 A water molecule can spin like a top, shake like a spring, or fly across the room — only one of these lets ice melt and water boil.一个水分子可以像陀螺一样转、像弹簧一样抖，也可以飞过整个房间——只有其中一种能让冰熔化、让水沸腾。

  1. Kinetic energy is the energy particles have because they are moving. Particles are always moving; the faster they move, the more kinetic energy they have. Temperature up → particles faster → kinetic energy up.动能是粒子因运动而具有的能量。粒子一直在运动；运动越快，动能越大。温度升高 → 粒子更快 → 动能增大。
  2. Rotational: the molecule spins in place; bond lengths and angles stay the same. Vibrational: atoms shake (bonds stretch and bend); bond lengths and angles change, but the molecule stays in the same place.转动：分子原地旋转，键长和键角不变。振动：原子来回抖动（键伸缩、弯曲），键长和键角改变，但分子留在原处。
  3. Translational: the whole molecule moves from one place to another (travels in a straight line until it hits something); bond lengths and angles do not change.平动：整个分子从一处移到另一处（沿直线运动，直到撞上东西），键长和键角不变。
  4. Translational energy matters most for phase changes: particles need enough freedom to pull away from each other. Melting and boiling happen when translational energy reaches a critical value.对相变最重要的是平动能：粒子需要足够的自由才能彼此拉开。平动能达到临界值时，就发生熔化和沸腾。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class graph says a solid gains "vibrational and translational" energy as it warms, while the table says solid particles "mostly vibrate in place". Many textbooks say solid particles only vibrate. On this test: solid = mostly vibrating in place; if asked what increases while a solid warms, give the class answer (vibrational, plus some translational).课堂图上说固体升温时获得「振动和平动」能量，而表格写的是固体粒子「主要在原地振动」。很多教科书说固体粒子只振动。本次考试：固体＝主要在原地振动；若问固体升温时增加的是什么，按课堂答案写（振动，加上少量平动）。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
For any "which motion?" question, ask two things: do the bonds change? does the molecule move to a new place? For "which matters most for phase change?" the answer is always translational.遇到「哪种运动」的题，问两件事：键变不变？分子换不换位置？问「对相变最重要的是哪种」，答案永远是平动。

Example 3例 3L4 Solve会想2 methods种解法
Walk through the heating curve of water and describe what the particles are doing in each of the five segments. Then explain why translational energy, not rotational or vibrational, decides when melting and boiling happen.沿着水的加热曲线，描述五段中粒子分别在做什么。再解释为什么决定何时熔化、何时沸腾的是平动能，而不是转动能或振动能。
  1. 1 Solid warming: particles packed close, mostly vibrating in place, vibrating harder as energy is added.1 固体升温：粒子紧密排列，主要在原地振动，加能量后抖得更厉害。
  2. 2 Melting: particles gain enough energy to break out of the solid arrangement and start sliding past each other; temperature constant.2 熔化：粒子获得足够能量，挣脱固体排列，开始彼此滑动；温度不变。
  3. 3 Liquid warming: particles move around more freely and faster; translational energy increases.3 液体升温：粒子更自由、更快地移动；平动能增加。
  4. 4 Boiling: particles get enough energy to escape the liquid completely; temperature constant. 5 Gas warming: particles far apart, racing around faster.4 沸腾：粒子获得足够能量完全逃离液体；温度不变。5 气体升温：粒子相距很远，跑得越来越快。
  1. Rotational: spins in place. Vibrational: shakes in place. Neither moves the molecule away from its neighbours.转动：原地旋转。振动：原地抖动。两者都不能让分子离开邻居。
  2. Translational motion carries the whole molecule from place to place. When it reaches a critical value, molecules can break out of the solid (melting) or the liquid (boiling).平动把整个分子从一处带到另一处。当平动能达到临界值时，分子就能挣脱固体（熔化）或液体（沸腾）。
Answer答案
Translational motion is the only one that moves molecules away from each other只有平动能让分子彼此远离

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Mixing up rotational and vibrational. Rotational: bonds unchanged, just spinning. Vibrational: bonds stretch and bend.把转动和振动搞混。转动：键不变，只是旋转。振动：键伸缩、弯曲。
  • Thinking vibrating moves a molecule across the container. Vibration happens in place; moving across is translational.以为振动会让分子穿过容器。振动是原地发生的；穿过容器是平动。
  • Saying kinetic energy increases during melting or boiling. On the flat parts the energy goes into separating particles, so average kinetic energy (temperature) stays the same.说熔化或沸腾时动能在增加。水平段上能量用来分开粒子，平均动能（温度）不变。

Which motion changes where the particle is?哪种运动会改变粒子所在的位置？
To change phase, particles must move away from their neighbours. Only translational motion moves a particle from place to place; spinning and vibrating happen in place.要相变，粒子必须离开周围的粒子。只有平动让粒子从一处移到另一处；旋转和振动都在原地发生。
  1. Particles need enough freedom to pull away from each other.粒子需要足够的自由才能彼此拉开。
  2. Only translational motion carries them away; when it reaches a critical value, they break out of the solid or liquid.只有平动能带着它们离开；平动能达到临界值时，它们就挣脱固体或液体。

Water at 100 °C is boiling on a stove. A student says: "The burner is adding energy, so the kinetic energy of the water molecules must be going up." Is the student right? Explain using kinetic energy and the heating curve.炉子上 100 °C 的水正在沸腾。某学生说：「炉子在加能量，所以水分子的动能一定在增加。」他说得对吗？用动能和加热曲线解释。
What does the thermometer do while water boils?水沸腾时温度计怎样？
No. The temperature stays at 100 °C, so the average kinetic energy (speed) of the molecules is not increasing. The added energy separates particles so they escape into the gas phase.不对。温度保持 100 °C，所以分子的平均动能（速度）没有增加。加入的能量用来分开粒子，让它们逃入气相。
  1. During boiling the curve is horizontal: temperature is constant at 100 °C.沸腾时曲线是水平的：温度恒定在 100 °C。
  2. Constant temperature means the particles are not moving faster on average, so average kinetic energy is not rising.温度不变说明粒子平均速度没有加快，平均动能没有增加。
  3. The energy is used to separate particles from the liquid into the gas.能量用来把粒子从液体中分开、变成气体。
