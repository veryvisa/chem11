Check for a new substance看有无新物质
  1. Melting changes only the phase, so the substance stays the same.熔化只改变相，物质本身不变。
  2. Cooling the liquid wax makes the same solid wax again.把液态蜡冷却，又得到同样的固态蜡。
Think of the opposite change想它的逆过程
  1. Freezing wax is the reverse of melting and gives back the original wax, so no new substance was made.凝固是熔化的逆过程，能得到原来的蜡，说明没有新物质生成。
🎯 Goal考查目标

Ice at −10 °C is heated steadily until it has all melted. (a) What happens to the temperature while the ice is melting? (b) Why does it behave this way?−10 °C 的冰被持续加热直到全部熔化。(a) 冰熔化期间温度怎样变化？(b) 为什么会这样？
(a) The temperature stays constant at 0 °C. (b) The added energy is used to loosen the forces holding the particles in the solid, not to make them move faster.(a) 温度保持在 0 °C 不变。(b) 加入的能量用来削弱把粒子固定在固体中的作用力，而不是让粒子运动变快。
Use the heating curve用加热曲线
  1. Melting is the first flat segment: the phase is changing, so the temperature is constant at 0 °C.熔化是曲线上第一段水平线：相在变化，温度恒定在 0 °C。
  2. Both solid and liquid are present during this segment.这一段固体和液体同时存在。
Use particle energy用粒子能量
  1. Energy goes into breaking the attractions between particles, so the average speed (and temperature) does not increase.能量用于克服粒子间的吸引力，平均速度（也就是温度）不增加。
