This is the brief on physical properties of matter. So, aluminum foil, copper wire, honey, a diamond ring, and the smell of perfume crossing a room are each a different property word on the test. Matching the object to the word gets you the marks. First, the core properties. Hardness is how well solids resist scratching.

Luster is how much light a solid reflects, shiny or dull. Malleability is being shaped into sheets without breaking. Ductility is being stretched into thin wire without breaking. Viscosity is a fluid's flow resistance. Diffusion is particles moving high to low concentration until evenly spread, reaching equilibrium.

Second, vapor is a gas formed when evaporating above 25 degrees Celsius. Vapor pressure is the pressure made by that vapor, measured in KPA. Crucial caveat, textbooks define vapor as the gas of a normally liquid or solid substance. For the test, use the class version. Third, worked examples.
