Atom, element, molecule, compound, ion原子、元素、分子、化合物、离子

  • An atom is the smallest particle of an element that still has that element's properties ("one gold particle"). Atoms are made of protons, neutrons and electrons; about 50 million lined up make 1 cm.原子＝仍保持元素性质的最小粒子（「一个金粒子」）。原子由质子、中子、电子构成；约 5000 万个排成一行才 1 cm 长。
  • An element is a pure substance made of only one type of atom — one TYPE, not one atom: OX2​, NX2​, HX2​ and a whole bar of gold are all elements.元素（单质）＝只由一种原子构成的纯净物——是一种「类型」，不是一个原子：OX2​、NX2​、HX2​ 和一整块金都是元素。
  • A molecule is 2 or more atoms (same OR different types) joined by bonds. A compound is a pure substance of 2 or more different types of atoms chemically combined in a fixed way (HX2​O, NaCl, NaHCOX3​).分子＝2 个或以上原子（同种或不同种）通过化学键连在一起。化合物＝由 2 种或以上不同原子按固定方式化学结合而成的纯净物（HX2​O、NaCl、NaHCOX3​）。
  • An ion is a particle with an electric charge — you will see a + or − in the formula (NOX3​X−, SOX4​X2−, NaX+).离子＝带电荷的粒子——化学式上会有 + 或 −（NOX3​X−、SOX4​X2−、NaX+）。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Thinking an element must be a single atom. OX2​ and FX2​ are elements (and molecules).以为元素必须是一个原子。OX2​、FX2​ 都是元素（也是分子）。
  • Missing the charge: SOX4​X2− is an ion, not a compound — always check for + or − first.漏看电荷：SOX4​X2− 是离子，不是化合物——先检查有没有 + 或 −。
  • Writing only one label when two apply: HX2​O, CHX4​, NHX3​ are both M and C.该写两个标签只写一个：HX2​O、CHX4​、NHX3​ 都是 M 和 C。
  • Calling a compound anything with 2+ atoms: a compound needs 2+ DIFFERENT types (NX2​ is not a compound).把含 2 个以上原子的都叫化合物：化合物需要 2 种以上「不同」原子（NX2​ 不是化合物）。

🎯 Write the letters (A, E, M, C, I) beside each formula, and give TWO letters when two apply — the class key expects "M, E" for OX2​ and "M, C" for HX2​O. Check for a charge before anything else.在每个化学式旁写字母（A、E、M、C、I），适用两个就写两个——讲义答案中 OX2​ 是「M、E」，HX2​O 是「M、C」。先看有没有电荷。

Pure substances, mixtures, solutions纯净物、混合物与溶液

  • Question 1 — how many phases? Homogeneous = one phase, looks the same everywhere (air, water, salt water, a piece of iron). Heterogeneous = more than one phase, you can see different parts (gravel, sand in water, a pencil, salad).第一问——几个相？均匀的＝一个相，处处看起来一样（空气、水、盐水、一块铁）。不均匀的＝不止一个相，能看出不同部分（碎石、沙和水、铅笔、沙拉）。
  • Question 2 (homogeneous only) — fixed composition? Pure substance = one substance, fixed formula (HX2​O, OX2​, Cu, sugar). Homogeneous mixture = solution = 2+ substances physically combined, amounts can vary (salt water, air, soda pop, water and alcohol).第二问（只对均匀的）——组成固定吗？纯净物＝一种物质、化学式固定（HX2​O、OX2​、Cu、糖）。均匀混合物＝溶液＝2 种以上物质物理混合，比例可变（盐水、空气、汽水、水和酒精）。
  • Pure substances split into elements (1 type of atom) and compounds (more than 1 type). Every heterogeneous mixture is a mechanical mixture (gravel, sand and iron filings, trail mix).纯净物分为元素（单质）（1 种原子）和化合物（不止 1 种原子）。所有不均匀的混合物都是机械混合物（碎石、沙和铁屑、什锦坚果）。
  • In a solution the solute is the substance that gets dissolved (salt) and the solvent does the dissolving (water).溶液里，溶质是被溶解的物质（盐），溶剂是起溶解作用的物质（水）。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling every clear liquid "pure". Salt water and soda pop look uniform but are solutions (mixtures).把澄清的液体都当纯净物。盐水、汽水看起来均匀，但是溶液（混合物）。
  • Putting compounds under mixtures because they contain 2+ elements. A compound has a fixed formula — it is a pure substance.因为化合物含 2 种以上元素就把它归为混合物。化合物化学式固定——是纯净物。
  • Mixing up solute and solvent: the solvent is usually the larger amount that does the dissolving (water).搞反溶质和溶剂：溶剂通常是量较多、起溶解作用的那一种（水）。

🎯 Expect to draw or fill in the classification tree from memory — practise it until you can sketch all eight boxes in under a minute. For any substance, ask the questions in order: phases? fixed composition? types of atom?常考默写或填写分类树——练到一分钟内画出全部八个框。遇到任何物质都按顺序问：几个相？组成固定吗？几种原子？
