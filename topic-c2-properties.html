📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Malleable = sheets; ductile = wires; hardness = resists scratching; lustre = shiny vs dull.可锻性＝成片；延性＝成丝；硬度＝抗刮；光泽＝亮或暗。
  • Viscosity = resistance to flow (honey high). Diffusion = high → low concentration to equilibrium.黏度＝抗流动（蜂蜜高）。扩散＝高→低浓度直到平衡。
  • Vapour = gas from evaporation above 25 °C (class). Vapour pressure in kPa; water 3.2 kPa.蒸气＝高于 25 °C 汽化形成的气体（讲义）。蒸气压单位 kPa；水为 3.2 kPa。

💡 Aluminum foil, copper wire, honey, a diamond ring, the smell of perfume crossing a room — each one is a different property word on your test. Match the object to the word and the marks are yours.铝箔、铜线、蜂蜜、钻戒、香水味飘满房间——每一个都对应考试里一个不同的性质词。把东西和词对上，分就到手了。

  1. Hardness = how well a solid resists scratching (diamond cannot be scratched; calcite can). Lustre = how much a solid reflects light: shiny (glassy, diamond-like, oily, silky) or dull (baking soda).硬度＝固体抵抗刮划的能力（钻石刮不动，方解石能刮出痕）。光泽＝固体表面反光的程度：有光泽（玻璃光泽、金刚光泽、油脂光泽、丝绢光泽）或暗淡无光（小苏打）。
  2. Malleability = can be hammered, rolled or shaped into sheets without breaking (aluminum → foil). Ductility = can be stretched or drawn into a thin wire without breaking (copper → wire).可锻性＝能被锤打、轧制成片而不破裂（铝→铝箔）。延性（可拉成丝）＝能被拉成细丝而不断（铜→铜线）。
  3. Viscosity = a fluid's resistance to flow: honey high, water low. Diffusion = particles moving from high to low concentration until evenly spread (equilibrium).黏度＝流体抵抗流动的程度：蜂蜜高，水低。扩散＝粒子从高浓度区移向低浓度区，直到分布均匀（达到平衡）。
  4. Vapour (class definition) = gas formed when a substance evaporates above room temperature (25 °C), e.g. steam over boiling water. Vapour pressure = pressure made by that vapour, a number in kPa (water: 3.2 kPa).蒸气（讲义定义）＝物质在高于室温（25 °C）时汽化形成的气体，如沸水上方的水蒸气。蒸气压＝蒸气产生的压强，是一个以 kPa 为单位的数值（水：3.2 kPa）。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class notes define vapour as gas formed by evaporation above room temperature (25 °C). Most textbooks define vapour as the gas of a substance that is normally a liquid or solid at room temperature, and note that evaporation happens at any temperature. On this test use the class version.课堂讲义把蒸气定义为高于室温（25 °C）时汽化形成的气体。多数教材的定义是：常温下本是液体或固体的物质所形成的气体，并指出任何温度下都会汽化。考试按讲义的说法。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
These come as "match the term to the example". Anchor each word to one class example: diamond/calcite, aluminum foil, copper wire, quartz/baking soda, honey/water, steam, 3.2 kPa.常考「把术语和例子配对」。每个词记一个讲义例子：钻石／方解石、铝箔、铜线、石英／小苏打、蜂蜜／水、水蒸气、3.2 kPa。

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
A drop of food colouring is placed in a still glass of water. After an hour the whole glass is evenly coloured. Name the process and explain it in terms of concentration.在一杯静止的水里滴一滴食用色素。一小时后整杯水颜色均匀。说出这个过程的名称，并用浓度来解释。
  1. At the start the dye particles are crowded in one spot → high concentration there, low everywhere else.开始时色素粒子挤在一处→那里浓度高，其他地方浓度低。
  2. Dye particles move from high to low concentration → diffusion.色素粒子从高浓度移向低浓度→扩散。
  3. When the colour is even, concentration is balanced → equilibrium.颜色均匀时，浓度平衡→达到平衡。
  1. Water and dye particles are always moving and bumping into each other.水和色素粒子一直在运动、互相碰撞。
  2. Random movement spreads the dye out; nobody stirs it. Once spread evenly, particles still move, but the colour no longer changes.随机运动让色素散开，并不需要搅拌。均匀后粒子仍在运动，但颜色不再变化。

Diffusion: high → low concentration until equilibrium扩散：从高浓度 → 低浓度，直到平衡

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Mixing up malleable and ductile. Malleable → sheets (think "mallet" = hammer). Ductile → wires.混淆可锻性和延性。可锻性（malleable）→成片（mallet＝锤子）；延性（ductile）→成丝。
  • Thinking high viscosity means "runny". High viscosity = thick and slow (honey); low viscosity = runny (water).以为黏度高就是「稀」。黏度高＝稠、流得慢（蜂蜜）；黏度低＝稀（水）。
  • Writing diffusion as low → high concentration. It always goes high → low.把扩散写成从低浓度到高浓度。方向永远是高→低。
  • Calling lustre "colour". Lustre is about how light reflects (shiny vs dull), not which colour it is.把光泽当成颜色。光泽说的是反光程度（亮或暗），不是什么颜色。

Someone opens a bottle of perfume at the front of a classroom with no fans running. A few minutes later students at the back smell it. Name the process and describe the direction the perfume particles move.在没开风扇的教室前面打开一瓶香水。几分钟后，后排学生闻到了香味。说出这个过程并描述香水粒子移动的方向。
Where is the concentration highest at the start?一开始哪里浓度最高？
Diffusion: perfume particles move from high concentration (near the bottle) to low concentration (back of the room) until evenly spread.扩散：香水粒子从高浓度（瓶子附近）移向低浓度（教室后面），直到分布均匀。
  1. No fan, so the spreading is due to particle movement alone → diffusion.没有风扇，所以扩散完全靠粒子运动→扩散。
  2. Direction: high → low concentration, toward equilibrium.方向：高浓度→低浓度，趋向平衡。
🎯 Goal考查目标
Apply diffusion to an everyday situation.把扩散用于日常情境。

Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity, diffusion, vapour硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度、扩散、蒸气Matter and its states物质与物质的状态

💡 Key idea解题思路
Near the bottle = high concentration; back of the room = low.瓶子附近＝高浓度；教室后面＝低浓度。

  • Concentration: high → low.浓度：高→低。

Copper can be drawn into wire; water cannot. Is ductility a property of liquids? Explain.铜能被拉成丝，水不能。延性（可拉成丝）是液体的性质吗？说明理由。
Can a liquid hold a wire shape?液体能保持丝的形状吗？
No. Ductility is a property of solids (mostly metals). A liquid has no definite shape, so it cannot be drawn into a wire.不是。延性是固体（主要是金属）的性质。液体没有固定形状，不能被拉成丝。
  1. A wire is a fixed shape; liquids take the shape of their container.丝是固定的形状；液体会随容器变形。
  2. So ductility applies to solids such as copper, not to water.所以延性适用于铜这样的固体，不适用于水。
🎯 Goal考查目标
Connect ductility to the solid state.把延性和固态联系起来。
📚 Knowledge used用到的知识
Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity, diffusion, vapour硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度、扩散、蒸气Matter and its states物质与物质的状态

🔗 Connecting ideas知识联系🔍 Checking & reasoning检验与推理
💡 Key idea解题思路
A wire must keep its own shape — only a solid has a definite shape.丝必须保持自己的形状——只有固体形状固定。
🔀 Other ways to think不同的思考方式
  • Use the states table: liquids have indefinite shape.用状态表：液体形状不固定。
  • Use the definition: drawn into a wire "without breaking" assumes a solid that holds together.用定义：「拉成丝而不断」的前提是能连成一体的固体。

An electrician needs a material for (a) long thin cables and (b) a scratch-resistant phone screen cover. Which property matters most for each, and which material from these fits: copper, glass-like sapphire, honey, baking soda?电工需要材料用于 (a) 细长的电缆，(b) 防刮的手机屏幕保护层。各自最看重哪种性质？在铜、蓝宝石玻璃、蜂蜜、小苏打中各选哪一个？
A cable is a wire.电缆就是丝。
(a) Ductility → copper. (b) Hardness → sapphire.(a) 延性→铜。(b) 硬度→蓝宝石。
  1. Cable: must be drawn into thin wire → ductility → copper.电缆：要拉成细丝→延性→铜。
  2. Screen: must resist scratches → hardness → sapphire (very hard).屏幕：要抗刮→硬度→蓝宝石（很硬）。
  3. Honey (liquid) and baking soda (powder) cannot do either job.蜂蜜（液体）和小苏打（粉末）两样都做不了。
🎯 Goal考查目标
Choose materials by property for a real job.按性质为实际用途选材料。
📚 Knowledge used用到的知识
Hardness, malleability, ductility, lustre, viscosity, diffusion, vapour硬度、可锻性、延性、光泽、黏度、扩散、蒸气Matter and its states物质与物质的状态
💪 Abilities锻炼的能力
🌍 Modelling建模📘 Concepts概念理解🧭 Choosing a strategy选择策略
💡 Key idea解题思路
Cable = wire → ductility. Screen = resists scratches → hardness.电缆＝丝→延性。屏幕＝抗刮→硬度。
🔀 Other ways to think不同的思考方式
  • Translate the job into a property word first, then pick the material.先把用途翻译成性质词，再选材料。
  • Eliminate: honey is a liquid, baking soda is a dull powder.排除法：蜂蜜是液体，小苏打是暗淡的粉末。
