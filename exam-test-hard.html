Q11 mark分L3 Connect会用
A lab report says: "A white solid formed and the tube grew warm, so the reaction was exothermic and a new substance was made." Which part is an observation?实验报告写道：「出现白色固体，试管变热，所以该反应是放热反应，并生成了新物质。」其中哪一部分是「观察」？
A. A white solid forming and the tube growing warm are both sensed. "Exothermic" and "a new substance was made" are interpretations.A. 出现白色固体和试管变热都是感官得到的。「放热」「生成了新物质」是解释。
Split at the word "so"以「所以」为界
  1. Before "so": what was seen and felt (observation). After "so": what it means (interpretation).「所以」之前：看到和摸到的（观察）；之后：它意味着什么（解释）。
Test with the senses用感官检验
  1. Can you see or feel it directly? A white solid and warmth: yes. "The reaction was exothermic": no, you infer it.能直接看到或感觉到吗？白色固体和变热：能；「该反应放热」：不能，是推断出来的。

Q145 marks分L4 Solve会想
A student wants to test whether stirring changes how fast salt dissolves in room-temperature water. (a) Write a testable hypothesis. (b) Say what data she should collect. (c) Describe a result that would reject the hypothesis.学生想检验搅拌是否影响盐在室温水中溶解的快慢。(a) 写出一个可检验的假设。(b) 她应该收集什么数据？(c) 描述一种会否定该假设的结果。
(a) If the water is stirred, then the salt will dissolve faster than in unstirred water. (b) Quantitative data: the time (in seconds) for the salt to dissolve completely in each case, using the same mass of salt, volume and temperature of water. (c) If the stirred and unstirred samples took about the same time (or the stirred one was slower), the hypothesis would be rejected.(a) 如果搅拌水，那么盐会比不搅拌时溶解得更快。(b) 定量数据：两种情况下盐完全溶解所需的时间（秒），盐的质量、水的体积和温度保持相同。(c) 如果搅拌与不搅拌所用时间差不多（或搅拌的更慢），假设就被否定。
Build it from the three parts按三部分搭建
  1. Hypothesis: one "if … then …" prediction that can be tested.假设：一句可检验的「如果…那么…」预测。
  2. Data: measured numbers, such as dissolving times, with other conditions kept the same.数据：测量得到的数值，例如溶解时间，其他条件保持不变。
  3. Rejecting result: times that contradict the prediction.否定的结果：与预测相矛盾的时间。
Work backwards from the test从检验倒推
  1. Decide what you will measure (time), then what outcome would disagree with your prediction, then phrase the prediction.先定要测什么（时间），再想什么结果与预测不符，最后把预测写成句子。

A student heats a white powder in a dish. It turns black and gives off a sharp-smelling smoke, and the black solid will not turn white again when cooled. A friend says this is only a physical change. Do you agree? Explain with two pieces of evidence.学生在蒸发皿中加热一种白色粉末，粉末变黑并冒出刺鼻的烟，冷却后黑色固体不能变回白色。朋友说这只是物理变化。你同意吗？用两条证据说明。
No, it is a chemical change. Evidence: (1) a new substance appears: a black solid and a smelly gas, which the white powder did not have; (2) it cannot easily be reversed by cooling.不同意，这是化学变化。证据：(1) 出现了新物质：黑色固体和有气味的气体，白色粉末本来没有；(2) 冷却后不能轻易恢复原状。
Look for new substances找新物质

Q173 marks分L4 Solve会想
Explain, in terms of particle motion and energy, why the temperature stays at the melting point while a solid is melting.从粒子运动和能量的角度，解释固体熔化时为什么温度保持在熔点不变。
In the solid, particles mostly vibrate in place. At the melting point, the added energy is used to overcome the attractions holding the particles in the solid arrangement and let them move past each other (translational motion). It is not increasing the average speed of the particles, so the temperature does not change until all the solid has melted.固体中的粒子主要在原位振动。到达熔点时，加入的能量用来克服把粒子固定在固体排列中的吸引力，使它们能相互滑动（平动）。能量没有用来提高粒子的平均速度，所以在固体全部熔化之前温度不变。
Follow the energy跟着能量走
  1. Temperature reflects the average kinetic energy of particles.温度反映粒子的平均动能。
  2. During melting the energy goes into breaking attractions instead, so the temperature is constant.熔化时能量改为用来破坏吸引力，所以温度不变。
Follow the motion跟着运动走
  1. Solid: vibration. Melting: particles gain enough translational freedom to slide. Liquid warming only starts when no solid is left.固体：振动。熔化：粒子获得足够的平动自由度而相互滑动。固体全部消失后，液体才开始升温。

Use the definition用定义
  1. Properties that depend on the amount (mass) are extensive and differ between pieces.与量有关的性质（质量）是广延性质，两块不同。
