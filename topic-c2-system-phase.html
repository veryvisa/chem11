📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • System = what you are studying. Phase = a uniform part of the system.体系＝研究对象。相＝体系中均一的部分。
  • Different states or layers = different phases. Gas mixtures = 1 phase. Solutions = 1 phase.不同状态或分层＝不同的相。气体混合物＝1 相。溶液＝1 相。
  • Class T/F: looks uniform → one phase (True); boundary → more than one phase (True).讲义判断题：看起来均匀→一个相（对）；有分界→不止一个相（对）。

💡 A glass of iced cola: how many phases? Count the visible parts — ice, liquid, bubbles — and you have the answer. Questions like this are quick marks once you know the counting rule.一杯加冰的可乐有几个相？数一数看得见的部分——冰、液体、气泡——答案就出来了。掌握数相的规则，这类题就是送分题。

  1. A system is the part of the universe you are studying — just what you are focusing on (water in a beaker, gas in a balloon). Name exactly the things being studied.体系＝你正在研究的那部分宇宙——就是你关注的对象（烧杯里的水、气球里的气体）。把正在研究的东西准确说出来。
  2. A phase is a part of a system that is uniform (the same) in composition and properties. Different states or visible layers = different phases.相＝体系中组成和性质都均一（处处相同）的部分。不同的状态或看得见的分层＝不同的相。
  3. Counting rule: ice + water = 2; oil + water = 2; salt water = 1 (looks the same everywhere). A mixture of gases is always 1 phase. A visible boundary means more than one phase.数相规则：冰＋水＝2；油＋水＝2；盐水＝1（处处看起来一样）。气体混合物永远是 1 相。看得见分界线就说明不止一个相。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class answer key marks "If two substances look uniform throughout, they must be one phase" as True. Many textbooks would add that looking uniform is a good sign but not proof. On this test answer True.课堂答案把「两种物质看起来处处均匀，就一定是一个相」判为「对」。很多教材会补充：看起来均匀是很好的迹象，但不是证明。考试按讲义答「对」。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
For phase counts, list every visibly different part in a column and number them. For "identify the system", copy all the things being studied — the class answers name both parts (e.g. "sugar & hot tea").数相时，把每个看起来不同的部分列成一列并编号。「找体系」题要把被研究的东西全写上——讲义答案都写两部分（如「糖和热茶」）。

Identify the system: (a) A student measures how the temperature changes when ice cubes melt in a cup of lemonade. (b) A scientist records the pressure of the gas given off in a sealed bottle of fermenting juice.找出体系：(a) 学生测量冰块在一杯柠檬水中熔化时温度的变化。(b) 科学家记录一瓶密封发酵果汁中放出的气体的压强。
  1. (a) The temperature change involves both the ice and the lemonade → system = ice cubes and lemonade.(a) 温度变化涉及冰块和柠檬水→体系＝冰块和柠檬水。

Example 2例 2L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
How many phases? A beaker holds water, a layer of vegetable oil on top, a few ice cubes, and sand at the bottom.有几个相？烧杯里有水、上面一层植物油、几块冰，底部还有沙子。
  1. Water (liquid) — 1. Oil layer (separate liquid) — 2. Ice (solid) — 3. Sand (a different solid) — 4.水（液体）——1。油层（另一种液体）——2。冰（固体）——3。沙（另一种固体）——4。
  2. Total: 4 phases.共 4 个相。
  1. Liquids: water and oil do not mix → 2 layers → 2 phases.液体：水和油不相溶→2 层→2 相。
  2. Solids: ice and sand are different substances with a boundary between them → 2 phases.固体：冰和沙是不同物质，有分界→2 相。
  3. 2+2=4 phases.2+2=4 个相。
Answer答案
4

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Counting substances instead of phases: salt water has two substances but only 1 phase.数物质而不是数相：盐水有两种物质，但只有 1 个相。
  • Saying two gases make two phases. Gases always mix into 1 phase.说两种气体形成两个相。气体总是混成 1 个相。
  • Naming only part of the system: for "sugar dissolving in hot tea" the system is the sugar AND the hot tea.只写出体系的一部分：「糖在热茶中溶解」的体系是糖和热茶两者。

A student shakes oil and vinegar salad dressing. Right after shaking it looks cloudy and mixed; after 10 minutes it separates into two layers. Count the phases after 10 minutes and explain whether shaking ever made it 1 phase.学生摇晃油醋沙拉汁。刚摇完看起来浑浊、混在一起；10 分钟后分成两层。数一数 10 分钟后的相数，并说明摇晃是否曾让它变成 1 个相。
Does cloudy mean uniform?浑浊算均一吗？
2 phases (oil layer and vinegar layer). Shaking never made 1 phase: the cloudy look shows oil droplets spread through the vinegar, so it was never uniform.2 个相（油层和醋层）。摇晃从未形成 1 个相：浑浊说明油滴散布在醋中，从来不是均一的。
  1. After 10 min: two layers with a boundary → 2 phases.10 分钟后：两层、有分界→2 个相。
  2. Cloudy is not the same as uniform; the oil and vinegar stayed separate parts, just mixed into droplets.浑浊不等于均一；油和醋始终是分开的部分，只是被打成了液滴。

💡 Key idea解题思路
Shaking only breaks the oil into tiny droplets; they are still separate parts.摇晃只是把油打成小油滴；它们仍是分开的部分。
🔀 Other ways to think不同的思考方式
  • Phase count after settling: two layers → 2 phases.静置后的相数：两层→2 相。
  • Mixture type: oil and vinegar do not dissolve in each other → heterogeneous; cloudiness = droplets you can see as not uniform.混合物类型：油和醋不互溶→不均匀；浑浊说明有液滴，并不均一。
