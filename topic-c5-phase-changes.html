📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • s→l melting · l→s freezing · l→g evaporation · g→l condensation固→液 熔化 · 液→固 凝固 · 液→气 汽化 · 气→液 液化
  • s→g sublimation · g→s deposition (both skip the liquid)固→气 升华 · 气→固 凝华（都跳过液态）
  • All are physical changes. Toward gas absorbs energy; away from gas releases it.都是物理变化。朝气态吸收能量；离开气态放出能量。

💡 Frost appears on a car window overnight without ever being liquid water — which of the six phase changes is that?一夜之间，车窗上结了霜，中间从来没有变成过液态水——这是六种相变中的哪一种？

  1. There are three states (solid, liquid, gas) and six arrows between them. Each pair goes both ways: melting ⇄ freezing, evaporation ⇄ condensation, sublimation ⇄ deposition.三种状态（固、液、气）之间有六个箭头，每两种状态之间来回各一个：熔化 ⇄ 凝固，汽化 ⇄ 液化，升华 ⇄ 凝华。
  2. Solid → liquid melting; liquid → solid freezing; liquid → gas evaporation; gas → liquid condensation; solid → gas sublimation (skips liquid); gas → solid deposition (skips liquid).固 → 液 熔化；液 → 固 凝固；液 → 气 汽化；气 → 液 液化（凝结）；固 → 气 升华（跳过液态）；气 → 固 凝华（跳过液态）。
  3. Every phase change is a PHYSICAL change: the substance stays the same, e.g. HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l). Read the state symbols (s), (l), (g) to name the change.每一种相变都是物理变化：物质不变，例如 HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l)。看状态符号 (s)、(l)、(g) 就能说出是哪种相变。
  4. Toward gas (melting, evaporation, sublimation) the substance absorbs energy to pull particles apart. Away from gas (freezing, condensation, deposition) it releases energy.朝气态方向（熔化、汽化、升华）要吸收能量，把粒子拉开；离开气态方向（凝固、液化、凝华）会放出能量。
🟡 How your class notes say it课堂讲义的说法
Your class diagram names liquid → gas "evaporation". Many textbooks call it "vaporization" and split it into evaporation (at the surface, below the boiling point) and boiling (throughout the liquid, at the boiling point). On this test, "evaporation" is the expected name; on the heating curve, the flat step at 100 °C is called "boiling".课堂笔记的三角图把「液 → 气」叫做「汽化（evaporation）」。很多教科书称之为 vaporization，并细分为表面汽化（低于沸点）和沸腾（整个液体内部、在沸点）。本次考试答「evaporation 汽化」即可；加热曲线上 100 °C 的平台则叫「沸腾」。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Draw the triangle (gas on top, solid bottom-left, liquid bottom-right) in the margin before you start — then every naming question is just "follow the arrow".考试开始先在页边画出三角图（气在上，固在左下，液在右下）——之后所有命名题都只是「沿箭头读」。

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做
Name the phase change: (a) frost forms on a car window (b) a puddle dries up after rain (c) dew forms on grass at dawn (d) an ice cube turns into water in a glass.说出相变名称：(a) 车窗上结霜 (b) 雨后水坑干了 (c) 清晨草上结露 (d) 杯中的冰块化成水。
  1. (a) Water vapour in the air → ice, skipping liquid: gas → solid = deposition.(a) 空气中的水蒸气 → 冰，跳过液态：气 → 固＝凝华。
  2. (b) Liquid water → water vapour: liquid → gas = evaporation.(b) 液态水 → 水蒸气：液 → 气＝汽化。
  3. (c) Water vapour → liquid drops: gas → liquid = condensation.(c) 水蒸气 → 小水滴：气 → 液＝液化（凝结）。
  4. (d) Solid → liquid = melting.(d) 固 → 液＝熔化。

Example 3例 3L3 Connect会用2 methods种解法
For each of the six phase changes, say whether the substance absorbs or releases energy. Then use the heating curve to explain why melting and boiling need heat added even though the temperature does not rise.六种相变中，各是吸收能量还是放出能量？再用加热曲线解释：为什么熔化和沸腾要加热，温度却不上升。
  1. Absorb energy: melting, evaporation, sublimation (particles move apart).吸收能量：熔化、汽化、升华（粒子分开）。
  2. Release energy: freezing, condensation, deposition (particles come together).放出能量：凝固、液化、凝华（粒子靠拢）。
  3. On the heating curve the flat lines (melting, boiling) need heat added: the energy pulls the particles apart instead of raising the temperature.加热曲线上的水平线（熔化、沸腾）都要加热：能量用来拉开粒子，而不是升高温度。
  1. On the heating curve, energy is added to melt ice at 0 °C and boil water at 100 °C.在加热曲线上，0 °C 熔化和 100 °C 沸腾时都要加入能量。
  2. Reading it backwards (cooling), condensing at 100 °C and freezing at 0 °C give that same energy out.倒过来读（冷却），100 °C 液化和 0 °C 凝固会放出同样多的能量。
Answer答案
absorb: melting, evaporation, sublimation; release: freezing, condensation, deposition吸收能量：熔化、汽化、升华；放出能量：凝固、液化（凝结）、凝华

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Mixing up sublimation and deposition. Sublimation starts as a SOLID (dry ice); deposition starts as a GAS (frost).把升华和凝华搞反。升华从固体开始（干冰）；凝华从气体开始（霜）。
  • Calling gas → liquid "evaporation" or liquid → gas "condensation". Condensation makes drops (liquid); evaporation makes gas.把气 → 液说成汽化，或把液 → 气说成液化。液化产生水滴（液体）；汽化产生气体。
  • Calling frost "freezing". Freezing starts from liquid; frost forms straight from water vapour → deposition.把结霜说成凝固。凝固从液体开始；霜是水蒸气直接变成的 → 凝华。
