Qualitative, quantitative, observation & interpretation定性、定量、观察与解释

  • Qualitative = no numbers, described with your senses (colour, smell, feel, sound, "bubbly", "hot"). Quantitative = a number from a measurement or a count (46.5 mL, 21.23 mg, 4 beakers).定性＝没有数字，用感官描述（颜色、气味、手感、声音、「冒泡」「很烫」）。定量＝来自测量或计数的数字（46.5 mL、21.23 mg、4 个烧杯）。
  • An observation is information you get directly with your senses. In this class an observation is always qualitative.观察＝直接用感官得到的信息。本课规定：观察一定是定性的。
  • An interpretation explains what an observation means. It is based on evidence but is an idea you cannot directly see ("a gas was produced, so a reaction happened").解释＝说明观察到的现象意味着什么。它有证据支撑，但本身是一个看不见的想法（「产生了气体，所以发生了反应」）。
  • A description lists properties using words and/or numbers, so it can be both qualitative and quantitative ("clear, colourless, 50 mL").描述＝用文字和／或数字列出性质，所以可以同时是定性和定量的（「澄清、无色、50 mL」）。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling a count qualitative: "There are 4 beakers" has a number, so it is quantitative.把计数当成定性：「有 4 个烧杯」含数字，是定量。
  • Writing a conclusion under Observations: "a gas was produced" or "a chemical reaction occurred" are interpretations; "bubbles formed" is the observation.把结论写进「观察」：「产生了气体」「发生了化学反应」是解释；「出现气泡」才是观察。
  • Calling a measurement an observation: in this class observations are qualitative only; a reading like 46.5 mL is quantitative information.把测量值当观察：本课观察只能是定性的；46.5 mL 这样的读数是定量信息。

🎯 Expect a two-column table: write Q/N or O/I next to each line. Circle any number first — a number means quantitative and rules out "observation".常考两栏表格：在每行旁写定性／定量或观察／解释。先圈出数字——有数字就是定量，而且不可能是「观察」。

Data, experiment, hypothesis, theory & law数据、实验、假设、理论与定律

  • Data = quantitative information measured in an experiment (numbers). Data can support or not support a hypothesis — it never proves it.数据＝实验中测得的定量信息（数字）。数据可以支持或不支持假设——但永远不能证明它。
  • An experiment is a test or procedure done to find a result and answer a question. A hypothesis is a single, unproven, testable educated guess, often written "If …, then …".实验＝为得到结果、回答问题而进行的测试或步骤。假设＝单一的、未经证实的、可检验的有根据的猜测，常写成「如果……那么……」。
  • A theory explains WHY nature behaves a certain way. It is built from many tested hypotheses and can change with new evidence (e.g. Cell Theory).理论＝解释「为什么」自然会这样。它由许多经过检验的假设建立起来，有新证据时可以修改（如细胞学说）。
  • A law describes WHAT happens but does not explain why. It is an experimentally established pattern, often written as an equation (e.g. Law of Conservation of Mass).定律＝描述「发生了什么」，不解释原因。它是实验反复确认的规律，常写成等式（如质量守恒定律）。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Writing "the data proves the hypothesis". Data only supports or does not support it.写「数据证明了假设」。数据只能支持或不支持假设。
  • Thinking a theory becomes a law after enough tests. They do different jobs: a theory explains why, a law describes what.以为理论检验够多次就变成定律。两者分工不同：理论解释为什么，定律描述是什么。
  • Calling a hypothesis a theory ("my theory is that sugar dissolves faster in hot water"). One untested guess is a hypothesis.把假设叫作理论（「我的理论是糖在热水里溶得更快」）。一个未检验的猜测只是假设。

🎯 For theory vs law, ask one question: does it say WHY? Yes → theory. For data questions, never write "proves" — write "supports" or "does not support".分理论和定律，只问一句：它说了「为什么」吗？说了→理论。数据题永远别写「证明」——写「支持」或「不支持」。
