Physical vs chemical properties物理性质与化学性质

  • Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space (solid, liquid, gas). Energy such as heat or light is NOT matter: it has no mass and takes up no space.物质是有质量、占有空间的一切东西（固体、液体、气体）。热、光这类能量不是物质：它们没有质量，也不占空间。
  • A physical property is something you can observe or measure WITHOUT changing the substance: colour, density, melting point, boiling point, hardness, lustre, viscosity, state at room temperature.物理性质：不改变物质本身就能观察或测量到的性质，例如颜色、密度、熔点、沸点、硬度、光泽、黏度、室温下的状态。
  • A chemical property describes how a substance can REACT to form a new substance: ability to rust, ability to burn (flammable), reacts with acid or water, tarnishes. To see it, a chemical change has to happen.化学性质：描述物质能怎样发生反应、生成新物质，例如会生锈、能燃烧（易燃）、与酸或水反应、会失去光泽（变暗）。要看到它，就必须发生化学变化。
  • Quick test: "Can I check this just by looking or measuring, and still have the same stuff?" Yes → physical. Words like rusts, burns, flammable, reacts, tarnishes → chemical.快速判断：「只靠看或量就能知道，而且东西还是原来的东西吗？」是 → 物理性质。出现生锈、燃烧、易燃、反应、变暗（失去光泽）等词 → 化学性质。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling colour a chemical property because colour can change in a reaction. Colour itself is seen by looking → physical; only "changes colour by reacting" points to chemical.因为反应时颜色会变，就把颜色当成化学性质。颜色本身用眼看就知道 → 物理性质；只有「通过反应变色」才指向化学。
  • Thinking "flammable" is physical because nothing is burning right now. A chemical property is the ABILITY to react; it does not have to be happening.因为现在没在烧，就认为「易燃」是物理性质。化学性质是发生反应的「能力」，不必正在发生。
  • Counting heat or light as matter. They are energy: no mass, no space taken up.把热或光当成物质。它们是能量：没有质量，也不占空间。

🎯 These come as quick "P or C" lists. Underline the verb: is/has/boils at/is hard → P; rusts/burns/reacts/tarnishes/flammable → C. Answer with the full word.这类题常以「P 还是 C」的列表出现。圈出动词：是／有／在……沸腾／坚硬 → 物理；生锈／燃烧／反应／变暗／易燃 → 化学。答题时写完整的词。

Extensive vs intensive properties广延性质与强度性质

  • Extensive and intensive are the two types of PHYSICAL property. The question is: does the value depend on how much substance you have?广延性质和强度性质是物理性质的两种类型。关键问题：这个数值跟物质的多少有没有关系？
  • Extensive property: depends on the amount. More stuff → bigger value. Examples: mass, volume, length, surface area, total mass of a group.广延性质：随物质的量变化。东西越多 → 数值越大。例如：质量、体积、长度、表面积、一组东西的总质量。
  • Intensive property: does NOT depend on the amount. Examples: density, melting point, boiling point, colour, temperature, viscosity.强度性质：与物质的量无关。例如：密度、熔点、沸点、颜色、温度、黏度。
  • Half-sample test: imagine cutting the sample in half. If the value halves → extensive. If it stays the same → intensive.「切一半」测试：想象把样品切成两半。数值也减半 → 广延性质；数值不变 → 强度性质。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling temperature extensive because a bigger pot "has more heat". Temperature is the same whether you have a cup or a bathtub at 40 °C → intensive.认为大锅「热量更多」所以温度是广延性质。一杯水和一缸水都可以是 40 °C → 温度是强度性质。
  • Calling density extensive because a big block is heavier. Heavier is mass; density stays the same.因为大块更重就把密度当成广延性质。更重的是质量，密度不变。
  • Forgetting the word "total": "total length" or "total mass" of something is extensive.忽略「总」字：某物的「总长度」「总质量」都是广延性质。

🎯 On a list question, write E or I quickly using the half-sample test. Watch for "total" (always E) and for temperature (always I).列表题用「切一半」快速写 E 或 I。注意「总……」（一定是 E）和温度（一定是 I）。

Physical vs chemical change物理变化与化学变化

  • Chemical change: NEW substances are formed. The substance becomes something different, and you cannot easily get the original back (burning, rusting, cooking, tarnishing).化学变化：生成了新物质。物质变成了别的东西，很难再变回原来的样子（燃烧、生锈、烹熟、变暗）。
  • Physical change: NO new substance. Only the form, state (phase) or appearance changes; it is still the same substance (melting, freezing, condensing, evaporating, cutting, dissolving).物理变化：没有新物质。只改变形状、状态（相）或外观，仍是同一种物质（熔化、凝固、液化、汽化、切割、溶解）。
  • The one real test: "Is there a new substance afterwards?" Clues of a chemical change — new colour, gas, solid (precipitate), heat or light given off — still need that test, because boiling also makes bubbles.唯一可靠的判断：「之后有没有新物质？」化学变化的线索——变色、产生气体、生成固体（沉淀）、放热或发光——仍要用这个标准核对，因为沸腾也会冒泡。
  • All six phase changes (melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, sublimation, deposition) are physical changes: HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l) is still water.六种相变（熔化、凝固、汽化、液化、升华、凝华）全是物理变化：HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l) 还是水。
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling boiling chemical because "a gas is produced". The gas is the same substance (water vapour) → physical. Ask WHAT the gas is.因为「产生了气体」就把沸腾当成化学变化。那是同种物质（水蒸气）→ 物理变化。要问气体「是什么」。
  • Calling breaking glass or shredding paper chemical because you cannot undo it. Hard to reverse is only a clue; no new substance → physical.因为碎玻璃、碎纸复原不了就判成化学变化。难以复原只是线索；没有新物质 → 物理变化。
  • Calling dissolving sugar chemical because the sugar "disappears". It is still sugar — evaporate the water and it comes back → physical.因为糖「不见了」就把溶解当化学变化。糖还是糖——把水蒸干就回来了 → 物理变化。

🎯 For every item, say the "new substance?" question in your head. Any phase-change word (melts, freezes, condenses, evaporates, boils) → physical, every time.每道题都在心里问一句「有新物质吗？」。看到相变词（熔化、凝固、液化、汽化、沸腾）→ 一律物理变化。
