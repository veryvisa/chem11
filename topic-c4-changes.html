📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Chemical change: new substance forms; hard to get the original back.化学变化：生成新物质；难以复原。
  • Physical change: same substance; only form, phase or appearance changes.物理变化：同种物质；只改变形状、相或外观。
  • All phase changes are physical. Bubbles, heat or colour are clues, not proof.所有相变都是物理变化。气泡、放热、变色只是线索，不是证据。

💡 Melting chocolate and burning toast both happen in a kitchen with heat — but only one of them gives you something you can never turn back into the original.巧克力熔化和吐司烤焦都在厨房里、都靠加热——但只有一个变出了再也变不回原样的东西。

  1. Chemical change: NEW substances are formed. The substance becomes something different, and you cannot easily get the original back (burning, rusting, cooking, tarnishing).化学变化：生成了新物质。物质变成了别的东西，很难再变回原来的样子（燃烧、生锈、烹熟、变暗）。
  2. Physical change: NO new substance. Only the form, state (phase) or appearance changes; it is still the same substance (melting, freezing, condensing, evaporating, cutting, dissolving).物理变化：没有新物质。只改变形状、状态（相）或外观，仍是同一种物质（熔化、凝固、液化、汽化、切割、溶解）。
  3. The one real test: "Is there a new substance afterwards?" Clues of a chemical change — new colour, gas, solid (precipitate), heat or light given off — still need that test, because boiling also makes bubbles.唯一可靠的判断：「之后有没有新物质？」化学变化的线索——变色、产生气体、生成固体（沉淀）、放热或发光——仍要用这个标准核对，因为沸腾也会冒泡。
  4. All six phase changes (melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, sublimation, deposition) are physical changes: HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l) is still water.六种相变（熔化、凝固、汽化、液化、升华、凝华）全是物理变化：HX2​O(s)​HX2​O(l) 还是水。
🎯 Test tip考试提示
For every item, say the "new substance?" question in your head. Any phase-change word (melts, freezes, condenses, evaporates, boils) → physical, every time.每道题都在心里问一句「有新物质吗？」。看到相变词（熔化、凝固、液化、汽化、沸腾）→ 一律物理变化。

Example 1例 1L2 Apply会做
Classify each as a physical or chemical change: (a) steam condenses on a mirror after a shower (b) paper burns and turns into ash (c) chocolate melts in your hand (d) a silver spoon tarnishes over time.判断物理变化还是化学变化：(a) 洗澡后水蒸气在镜子上液化 (b) 纸燃烧变成灰 (c) 巧克力在手里熔化 (d) 银勺子日久变暗。
  1. (a) Water vapour → liquid water: still HX2​O → physical.(a) 水蒸气 → 液态水：还是 HX2​O → 物理变化。
  2. (b) Ash and smoke are new substances → chemical.(b) 灰和烟是新物质 → 化学变化。
  3. (c) Solid chocolate → liquid chocolate, same stuff → physical.(c) 固态巧克力 → 液态巧克力，还是同样的东西 → 物理变化。
  4. (d) The dark coating is a new substance formed with air → chemical.(d) 黑色表层是与空气反应生成的新物质 → 化学变化。

Bubbles form in a pot of boiling water, and also when an antacid tablet is dropped into cold water. Classify each change.一锅水沸腾时会冒泡；把一片抗酸泡腾片丢进冷水也会冒泡。分别判断这两个变化。

Example 3例 3L4 Solve会想2 methods种解法
A lit candle: wax near the wick turns liquid, then the liquid wax travels up the wick and burns, giving heat, light, carbon dioxide and water vapour. Name every change and classify each one.点燃的蜡烛：灯芯旁的蜡变成液体，液态蜡顺着灯芯上升并燃烧，放出热和光，生成二氧化碳和水蒸气。找出其中每一个变化并分类。
  1. Solid wax → liquid wax (melting): still wax → physical.固态蜡 → 液态蜡（熔化）：还是蜡 → 物理变化。
  2. Liquid wax moving up the wick: only position changes → physical.液态蜡沿灯芯上升：只是位置变化 → 物理变化。
  3. Wax burning → carbon dioxide + water vapour, with heat and light: new substances → chemical.蜡燃烧 → 二氧化碳＋水蒸气，并放热发光：生成新物质 → 化学变化。
  1. Carbon dioxide and water are different substances from wax, so at least one chemical change happens: burning.二氧化碳和水与蜡是不同物质，所以至少有一个化学变化：燃烧。
  2. The puddle of liquid wax, if cooled, becomes solid wax again — so melting is physical.那一小摊液态蜡冷却后又变回固态蜡——所以熔化是物理变化。
Answer答案
melting and rising: physical; burning: chemical熔化和膨胀上升：物理变化；燃烧：化学变化

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Calling boiling chemical because "a gas is produced". The gas is the same substance (water vapour) → physical. Ask WHAT the gas is.因为「产生了气体」就把沸腾当成化学变化。那是同种物质（水蒸气）→ 物理变化。要问气体「是什么」。
  • Calling breaking glass or shredding paper chemical because you cannot undo it. Hard to reverse is only a clue; no new substance → physical.因为碎玻璃、碎纸复原不了就判成化学变化。难以复原只是线索；没有新物质 → 物理变化。
  • Calling dissolving sugar chemical because the sugar "disappears". It is still sugar — evaporate the water and it comes back → physical.因为糖「不见了」就把溶解当化学变化。糖还是糖——把水蒸干就回来了 → 物理变化。

Shredded pieces are still paper (no new substance) → physical. Burning turns paper into ash, smoke and gases — new substances → chemical.碎纸片仍是纸（没有新物质）→ 物理变化。燃烧把纸变成灰、烟和气体——新物质 → 化学变化。
  1. Shredding changes only size and shape; every piece is still paper → physical.撕碎只改变大小和形状，每片都还是纸 → 物理变化。
  2. Burning produces ash, smoke and gases that are not paper → chemical.燃烧生成灰、烟和气体，都不是纸 → 化学变化。

In a lab, two clear colourless solutions are mixed. A yellow solid appears and settles, and the beaker feels warm. (a) Write two observations. (b) Write one interpretation. (c) Physical or chemical change?实验中把两种无色透明溶液混合，出现黄色固体并沉到底部，烧杯摸起来变热。(a) 写出两条观察。(b) 写出一条解释。(c) 这是物理变化还是化学变化？
Observation = senses only. Interpretation = what it means.观察＝只写感官所得。解释＝它意味着什么。
(a) A yellow solid formed; the beaker became warm. (b) A precipitate formed, so a reaction occurred (or: it is exothermic because heat was released). (c) Chemical change.(a) 生成黄色固体；烧杯变热。(b) 生成了沉淀，说明发生了反应（或：放出热量，所以是放热反应）。(c) 化学变化。
  1. Observations: yellow solid appeared and settled; beaker felt warm.观察：出现黄色固体并沉降；烧杯摸起来热。
  2. Interpretation: the solid is a precipitate, a new substance, so a chemical reaction occurred; heat was released, so it is exothermic.解释：固体是沉淀，是新物质，所以发生了化学反应；放出了热，所以是放热反应。
  3. A new substance formed → chemical change.生成了新物质 → 化学变化。
